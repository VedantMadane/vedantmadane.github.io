### What this post is about
**Read-your-writes** guarantees that a client sees its own successful updates on subsequent reads despite replica lag and caches. It is cheaper than making every reader linearizable. It is not isolation inside one engine transaction.

### Topics
1. Stale self after write  
2. Sticky primary sessions  
3. Version tokens  
4. Cache and edge coherence  
5. Session versus cross-device scope  
6. Offline optimistic echo  
7. Multi-region sticky paths  
8. Stale-self UX metrics  
9. Not full linearizability  
10. Isolation versus session versus replicas  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **sticky route after write**: primary or caught-up  
२. **return version watermarks**: honor on read  
३. **invalidate or version caches**: the user will hit  
४. **promise RYW in-session first**: document cross-device  
५. **reconcile offline echoes**: with server ack  
६. **region-sticky after a geo write**: no instant global myth  
७. **metric stale-self rate**: as a UX objective  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- load-balance the next read randomly right after write  
- edge-cache a profile page without bust on edit  
- claim linearizability while only doing sticky RYW
