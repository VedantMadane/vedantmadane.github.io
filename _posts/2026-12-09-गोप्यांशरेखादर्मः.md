3. Allowlist not denylist  
4. Tokens and keyed hashes  
5. Redact at source

7. Tickets and chat  
8. Canary PII tests  
9. Short sensitive retention  
10. Headers vs redaction vs soft delete

<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **classify fields by sensitivity**: default safe  
२. **allowlist log/trace fields**: not denylist hope  
३. **prātīka or keyed hash for correlation**: no raw email keys  
४. **redact in logger libraries**: not only SIEM  
५. **include exceptions and spans**: same policy  
६. **guard tickets and chat pastes**: human path  
७. **canary PII in CI/prod detectors**: prove  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- log full request bodies by default in prod  
- rely only on SIEM regex after indexing  
- use raw email as the join key in every log line
