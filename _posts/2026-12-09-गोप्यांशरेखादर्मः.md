### What this post is about
**गोप्यांशरेखा** keeps personal and secret data out of lines traces tickets and other telemetry so observation does not become a second breach warehouse.

### Topics
1. Writings as a weapon  
2. Field class  
3. Allowlist not ban-list  
4. प्रतीकम् for joins  
5. Omit at the source  
6. Traces and stacks  
7. Tickets and talk  
8. Marker tests  
9. Short sensitive retain  
10. Header vs line vs row  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

### English

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **classify fields by sensitivity**: default safe  
२. **allowlist log and trace fields**: not ban-list hope  
३. **प्रतीकम् or keyed digest for joins**: no raw name keys  
४. **redact in writer libraries**: not only a late strip  
५. **include exceptions and spans**: same policy  
६. **guard tickets and chat pastes**: human path  
७. **marker strings on the build path**: prove  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- write full request bodies by default in production  
- rely only on a late central strip after indexing  
- use a raw letter as the join key in every line
