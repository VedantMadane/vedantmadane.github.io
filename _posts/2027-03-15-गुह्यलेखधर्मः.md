### What this post is about
Secret-writing dharma is honest custody of dark facts. Never publish secrets, keep keys separate from code, bound who may read, set time limits and destroy stale secret material.

### Topics
1. Why keep secret writing dark
2. Hold the secret seed apart
3. Method: guard separate bound
4. Attach a time bound on secrets
5. Bound who may read
6. Burn stale secrets on a beat
7. Name the failure
8. Write the secret bound first
9. Not open-secret pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
गुह्यलेखधर्मः = dharma of secret writing (keys and sealed facts).

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **keep secrets out of open logs**: dark  
२. **hold keys apart from app seeds**: apart  
३. **bound who may read secrets**: access  
४. **set time bounds on secret life**: time  
५. **burn stale secrets on a schedule**: burn  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Commit secrets into open trees  
- Mix keys with ordinary app seeds  
- Grant all roles full secret read  
- Never rotate or burn stale secrets  
- Hide secret leaks from operators
