### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **scale on a true metric**: not on noise  
२. **bind min and max**: no endless spawn  
३. **cool between steps**: thrash is a war  
४. **watch product fruit**: green boxes can be red money  
५. **warm before zero**: cold start is user pain  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- scale on a vanity metric  
- no max so the bill explodes  
- treat scale as the only bug-fix
