<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम्
१. **माप्यसङ्केतः**: scale on meaningful load not vanity CPU only  
२. **सीमान्यूनोच्चम्**: min max replicas explicit  
३. **शीतलता**: cooldown after scale  
४. **दोलनरोधः**: anti-thrash hysteresis  
५. **शीतरम्भविवेकः**: cold start budget  
६. **पूर्वानुमानसन्धिः**: schedule scale for known peaks  
७. **लागतसत्यम्**: scale out has a bill  

### त्याज्यम्
- scale to zero on sticky sessions without sticky plan  
- react only on CPU when IO-bound  
- no max cap infinite spend
