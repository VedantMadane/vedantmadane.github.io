### What this post is about
**जागरणधर्मः** is when a sound is allowed to wake a human.

### Topics
1. Sound without meaning
2. Actionable only
3. A route not a crowd
4. Bound repeats
5. Method of a wake
6. Page is not the status page
7. Guard night sleep
8. Owner on the sound
9. Probe is not a page
10. Sight versus wake
11. Five limbs
12. Close

Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

२. **route to one owner**: not the whole company
