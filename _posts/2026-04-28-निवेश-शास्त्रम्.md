## १. मूल-सिद्धान्त-प्रकरणम् (Chapter on Core Principles) – अनुष्टुभ्

### १.१ मङ्गलाचरणं तथा भूमिका (Invocation & Introduction)

**मूल्ये सर्वं ज्ञानं विलीयते** – In the price, all information is dissolved/incorporated.  
**सञ्चयस्य विभागो हि** – The allocation of the portfolio indeed.  
**सर्व-निर्णय-नायकः** – Is the leader/primary of all decisions (Asset Allocation).

  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">मार्गाङ्को विश्व-कम्पकृत् सञ्चये ज्ञातुमर्हति ॥ ३ ॥</span>

<summary>शब्दार्थाः</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

**लाभ-सूचको** – Is the indicator of reward.  
**वैविध्यं हरते दोषम्** – Diversification removes the flaw (idiosyncratic risk).  
**मार्गाङ्को** – Beta. (*Mārga-aṅka* = market trajectory coefficient).  
**विश्व-कम्पकृत्** – Which causes systemic vibration (Systematic Risk).  
**सञ्चये ज्ञातुमर्हति** – One ought to know in the portfolio.

</div>
</details>

**कालमानं च वक्रता** – Duration and convexity.  
**लाभांश-मान-तन्त्रेण** – By the Dividend Discount Model (*Lābhāṃśa* = dividend + *māna-tantra* = measuring model).

**मानवानां विकल्पेन** – By the bias/irrationality of humans.

  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">अन्तर-प्रापणं नास्ति साम्य-सूत्रे व्यवस्थिते ॥ १७ ॥</span>

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

**समतां गतौ** – Have reached parity.  
**अन्तर-प्रापणं नास्ति** – Arbitrage does not exist.
