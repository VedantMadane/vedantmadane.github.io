### What this post is about
Group-service dharma is honest bulkhead isolation. Keep service groups separate, watch group fruit on a beat, stop the path on a faulty group and never treat one-group ruin as shared fate.

### Topics
1. Why group service
2. Keep groups separate
3. Method: hold group separate watch
4. Watch group fruit
5. Stop path on faulty group
6. Write the group bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Guard service when group is proven
9. Not single-ruin pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
समूहसेवाधर्मः = dharma of group-service (bulkhead isolation).
