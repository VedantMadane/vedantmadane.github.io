### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **name topics as a contract**: no silent wildcards  
२. **choose broadcast or key or pattern**: on purpose  
३. **idempotent handlers**: assume at-least-once  
४. **isolate slow subscribers**: per-sub backpressure  
५. **lock who may publish or listen**: open topic is a foot-gun  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one slow listener backing up everyone  
- open topic with no rights  
- promise exactly-once as bus magic
