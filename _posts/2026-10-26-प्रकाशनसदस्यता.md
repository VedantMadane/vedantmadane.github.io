### What this post is about
**Publish-subscribe** delivers each published message to many independent subscribers.

### Topics covered
1. One-to-many delivery  
2. Topic and routing contracts  
3. Fanout vs routed topologies  
4. At-least-once and idempotent handlers  
5. Slow subscriber isolation  
6. Per-subscription DLQ  
7. Schema on payloads  
8. Publish/subscribe ACLs  
9. Lag and depth metrics  
10. Queue vs pub/sub vs outbox  

### Sanskrit title
**प्रकाशनसदस्यता** = *prakāśana-sadasyatā* "publication-membership" (pub/sub).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **named topics**: documented routing  
२. **idempotent subscribers**: duplicate safe  
३. **isolate slow subs**: private buffers  
४. **DLQ per sub**: with alerts  
५. **schema validate**: on produce  
६. **ACL both ways**: pub and sub  
७. **per-sub lag metrics**: ops visibility  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one shared queue for all subscriber speeds  
- open publish to any service account  
- assume exactly-once without handler care
