### What this post is about
**Distributed consensus** lets replicas agree on one ordered history so a crash or partition cannot invent a second truth.

### Topics
1. Why many places need one truth
2. Elect a term-bound leader
3. Ballot or term numbers
4. Majority quorum
5. Propose vote commit
6. Log prefix match
7. Split-brain ban
8. Membership change
9. Stale leader fence
10. Not two-phase commit or gossip
11. Five limbs
12. Close

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **one ordered log**: not per-node fiction  
२. **majority of the current set**: not a lone writer  
३. **term or ballot on every grant**: stale leader must lose  
४. **match prefixes before append**: never fork silently  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- accept writes from two live leaders  
- change membership without a joint term
