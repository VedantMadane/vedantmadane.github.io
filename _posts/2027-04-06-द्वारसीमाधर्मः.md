### What this post is about
Gate-bound dharma is honest admission control at the door. Measure gate load, watch gate fruit on a beat, stop entry when the gate is full and never treat endless gate as safe.

### Topics
1. Why bound the gate
2. Measure gate do not run endless
3. Method: hold bound measure watch
4. Watch gate fruit
5. Stop entry when full
6. Write the gate bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Open gate when empty is proven
9. Not endless-gate pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
द्वारसीमाधर्मः = dharma of gate-bound (admission control).
