### What this post is about
Same-time dharma is honest ordering across nodes. Stamp facts with clock seeds, compare periodically, reject stale-as-new and name clock faults.

### Topics
1. Why keep the time-slice
2. Align when paths meet
3. Method: stamp compare reject-stale
4. Clock seed on every write
5. Compare on a beat
6. Stale is not new
7. Bound decision windows
8. Name clock fault
9. Not wall-clock pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
समसमयधर्मः = dharma of same-time (shared ordering clocks).

१. **attach clock seeds to writes**: stamp  
२. **compare replicas on a schedule**: compare

४. **bound decision wait on time**: bound  
५. **name clock skew faults**: name  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid

- No version seed on writes  
- Accept older write over newer without rule  
- No periodic order compare
