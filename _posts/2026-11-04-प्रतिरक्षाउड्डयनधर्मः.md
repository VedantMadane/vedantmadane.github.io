<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Hedged requests** launch a delayed backup attempt when the first is slow and keep the first success. They spend extra load to cut the tail. They are not retries (which follow hard failure) and not singleflight (which collapses identical work).

### Topics
1. Tail-cutting motive  
2. Percentile-delayed start  
3. Cancel the loser  
4. Prefer idempotent reads  
5. Method of cancel  
6. Amplification budgets  
7. Diverse replicas  
8. Win rate versus extra load  
9. Objective-gated use  
10. Retry versus hedge versus singleflight  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **delay hedge to a high percentile**: not instant double  
२. **cancel losers**: orphans are residual load  
३. **prefer idempotent reads**: guard writes with keys  
४. **cap hedge amplification**: per call and globally  
५. **diverse backends**: avoid correlated stuck tails  
६. **track win rate and cost**: honest trade  
७. **enable where tails hurt**: not as fashion  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- always dual-send every request  
- leave loser hedges running  
- hedge non-idempotent pays without keys
