**Hedged requests** launch a delayed backup attempt when the first is slow and keep the first success to cut tail latency.

1. Tail cutting motive
2. Percentile-delayed start
3. Cancel the loser
4. Prefer idempotent reads
5. Method of cancel
6. Amplification budgets
7. Diverse replicas
8. Win rate versus extra load
9. Objective-gated use
10. Retry versus hedge versus singleflight
11. Five limbs
12. Close

१. **delay hedge to a high percentile**: not instant double
