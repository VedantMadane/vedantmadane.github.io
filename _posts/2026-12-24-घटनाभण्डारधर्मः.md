<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **append never silent rewrite**: integrity  
२. **key streams by entity**: order  
३. **consumers idempotent**: replay safe  
४. **retain with policy**: cost+law  
५. **do not SQL the log as product DB**: wrong tool  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Editable event history  
- No stream keys  
- Non-idempotent consumers  
- Infinite retain forever
