<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **choose the layer**: transit is not rest  
२. **negotiate with the client**: avoid double-bind  
३. **skip tiny payloads**: processor can cost more  
४. **pick a latency-safe level**: max is rarely free  
५. **measure ratio and tail**: pretty ratio can lose  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- max level on every request  
- double-compress already bound images  
- enable without before/after numbers
