### What this post is about
**Mutual TLS** authenticates both sides of a connection with certificates so service identity is proven not assumed from network location.

### Topics covered
1. Beyond private-net trust
2. Workload identity issuance
3. Short-lived rotation
4. Authorize after authenticate
5. Fail-closed verify
6. Mesh vs app terminate
7. Clock skew hygiene
8. Handshake error metrics
9. Break-glass access
10. Headers vs mutual proof vs key life

### Sanskrit title
**पारस्परिकTLSधर्मः** keeps the series title. Verses use **उभयप्रमाणम्**.

१. **workload identities**: auto rotate  
२. **deny expired peers**: hard  
३. **authz by identity and method**: not any-paper god
