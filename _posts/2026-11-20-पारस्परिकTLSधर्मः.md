### What this post is about
**Mutual TLS** authenticates both sides of a connection with certificates so service identity is proven not assumed from network location.

### Topics covered
1. Beyond private-IP trust  
2. Workload identity issuance  
3. Short-lived rotation  
4. Authorize after authenticate  
5. Fail-closed verify  
6. Mesh vs app terminate  
7. Clock skew hygiene  
8. Handshake error metrics  
9. Break-glass access  
10. Headers vs mTLS vs key life  

### Sanskrit title
**पारस्परिकTLSधर्मः** = *pārasparika-TLS-dharmaḥ* "dharma of mutual TLS".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **workload identities**: auto rotate  
२. **deny expired peers**: hard  
३. **authz by identity+method**: not any-cert god  
४. **fail closed verify**: prod default  
५. **one terminate layer clear**: mesh or app  
६. **NTP hygiene**: avoid skew flaps  
७. **handshake metrics by reason**: fast RCA  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- trust flat network without peer certs  
- optional mTLS forever in prod  
- accept expired certs to stop pages
