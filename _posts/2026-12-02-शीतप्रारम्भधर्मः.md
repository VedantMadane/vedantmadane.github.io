<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Cold start** is the first-serve penalty when a new instance must boot and warm before it behaves like a steady replica.

### Topics covered
1. User-visible first-call tax  
2. Multi-cause boot path  
3. Min warm instances  
4. Readiness after warmup  
5. Slim images and lazy deps  
6. Platform snapstart  
7. Separate cold/warm latency  
8. Pre-warm deploy waves  
9. Warm pools and DNS  
10. Autoscale birth vs drain death  

### Sanskrit title
**शीतप्रारम्भधर्मः** = *śīta-prārambha-dharmaḥ* "dharma of cold beginning".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **keep warm floor on critical paths**: cost vs latency  
२. **ready only after warmup probes**: not on bind  
३. **slim boot critical path**: lazy rare features  
४. **split cold vs warm p95**: dashboards  
५. **pre-warm new pods before full traffic**: deploys  
६. **warm pools and DNS in startup**: not first request  
७. **treat scale-to-zero as product choice**: not free  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- mark ready on port open with empty pools  
- blend cold and warm latency as one SLO  
- replace entire warm fleet with cold pods at once
