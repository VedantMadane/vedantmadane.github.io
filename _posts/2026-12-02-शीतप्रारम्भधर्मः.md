### What this post is about
**शीतप्रारम्भ** is the extra latency and origin load when a new seat cache replica or model boots empty and the first public request pays to heat it.

### Topics
1. First-request tax  
2. Prefetch the cache  
3. Readiness not a port ping  
4. Stampede on empty  
5. Heat-first method  
6. Seed a new replica  
7. First-write clocks  
8. Soak before full weight  
9. Model-seat pre-load  
10. Cache versus boot versus replica  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close

Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **warmup before the pool**: not user-paid heat  
२. **readiness that proves warm state**: not port-only live  
३. **single-flight an empty miss**: bound waiters  
४. **seed a new region or replica**: before traffic  
५. **soak or half-weight a new fleet**: not instant full  
६. **pre-load model seats**: synthetic first load  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- route production at a just-booted empty cache  
- let every miss hit origin together  
- treat first-compile time as ordinary p99
