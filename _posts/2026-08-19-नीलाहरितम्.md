### ग्राह्यम्
१. **द्विभूमिः**: blue and green both deployable  
२. **एकसक्रिययातायातम्**: one color serves users at a time  
३. **स्वास्थ्यपूर्वस्विचः**: new color ready before cutover  
४. **स्विचः**: router/DNS/gateway flip is atomic-ish  
५. **प्रत्यावर्तनम्**: flip back without rebuild panic  
६. **कृपासन्धिः**: drain losers after flip  
७. **समता**: config parity except version under test  

### त्याज्यम्
- half-migrated shared mutable state without a story  
- cutover before readiness is green  
- no rollback path because green deleted blue too early
