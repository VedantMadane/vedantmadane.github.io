### What this post is about
**A sidecar is a helper process colocated with the app that takes cross-cutting duties so many languages share one implementation.**

### Topics covered
1. Why a helper  
2. Shared birth and death  
3. Summed resources  
4. Blast radius  
5. Keep business in the body  
6. Lock admin ports  
7. Version matrix  
8. Fail open or closed  
9. Observe the helper  
10. Door vs side  

### Sanskrit title
**पार्श्वयानधर्मः**.
