### What this post is about
**A sidecar is a helper process colocated with the app that takes cross-cutting duties so many languages share one implementation.**

### Topics covered
1. Why a helper  
2. Shared birth and death  
3. Summed resources  
4. Blast radius  
5. Keep business in the body  
6. Lock admin ports  
7. Version matrix  
8. Fail open or closed  
9. Observe the helper  
10. Door vs side  

### Sanskrit title
**पार्श्वयानधर्मः**.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **define start and stop order**: shared volumes  
२. **schedule on the sum**: app plus helper  
३. **roll the helper carefully**: one bug hits every language  
४. **bind admin to local**: not the LAN  
५. **pick fail-closed or fail-open**: say it aloud  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- forgotten helper memory  
- admin port on every pod address  
- helper version skew with no matrix
