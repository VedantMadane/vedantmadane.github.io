### What this post is about
A sudden hard kill corrupts data, leaves clients hanging and drops in-flight transactions. Graceful shutdown stops new incoming requests, drains existing work within a bound, flushes pending buffers, notifies clients cleanly and terminates without panic.

### Topics
1. Why graceful shutdown matters
2. Stop incoming traffic on signal
3. Five-step shutdown progression
4. Finish in-flight work
5. Bounded drain window
6. Flush memory and buffers
7. Drain transport connections
8. Clean farewell vs abrupt cut
9. Avoid abrupt termination
10. Five limbs of shutdown
11. Fearless and resilient service
12. The final seal

### Sanskrit title
शान्तविरामधर्मः = dharma of graceful shutdown.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **संज्ञां प्राप्य द्वारं संवृणु**: close ingress first upon termination signal  
२. **आरब्धकार्यं परिसमापय**: complete in-flight requests gracefully  
३. **कालसीमां प्रतीक्षस्व**: bound drain duration with a timeout  
४. **स्मृतिस्रावं कुरु**: flush in-memory buffers to persistent log  
५. **शान्त्या निर्गच्छ**: close connections cleanly before exit  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Sudden SIGKILL without giving workers time to complete  
- Accepting new ingress requests while dying  
- Unbounded draining that hangs the deployment forever  
- Discarding uncommitted write buffers on exit  
- Dropping client TCP connections without fin/farewell
