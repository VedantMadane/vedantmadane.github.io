### What this post is about
Seed-guard dharma is honest idempotent identity. Hold the same seed across retry, watch seed fruit on a beat, stop write on uneven seed and never treat seedless write as safe.

### Topics
1. Why guard the seed
2. Hold same seed do not forget
3. Method: hold same watch
4. Watch seed fruit
5. Stop write on uneven seed
6. Write the seed bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Guard seed when same is proven
9. Not seedless-write pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
बीजरक्षाधर्मः = dharma of seed guard (idempotency key).
