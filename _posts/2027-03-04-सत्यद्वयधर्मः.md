### What this post is about
Split-brain is two live truths for one cluster. Dharma is single leadership, witness logs, careful rejoin and honest naming of the partition  -  never silent dual masters.

### Topics
1. Why dual truth burns
2. Fix one leader
3. Method: one truth one head
4. Choose at death-time
5. Keep a witness
6. Remember the clock slice
7. Measured rejoin
8. Name the partition
9. Not dual-master pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
सत्यद्वयधर्मः = dharma of the dual-truth (split-brain).
