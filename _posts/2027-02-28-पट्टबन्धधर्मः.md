### What this post is about
A lease is ownership with an expiry. Distributed work stays honest when every holder knows the clock, renews on purpose and yields when the bond is dead.

### Topics
1. Why time-bound hold
2. Write the bound first
3. Method: bind by clock
4. Single holder
5. Death and reclaim
6. Measured renewal
7. Honest advertise
8. Remember the slice
9. Not eternal lock
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
पट्टबन्धधर्मः = dharma of the time-bound lease-bond.
