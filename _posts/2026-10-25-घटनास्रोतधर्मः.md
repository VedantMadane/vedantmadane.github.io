### What this post is about
**Event sourcing stores every state change as an immutable event. Current state is a projection of the log not the only record.**

### Topics covered
1. Why history  
2. Never rewrite the past  
3. Aggregate bounds  
4. Read projections  
5. Snapshots  
6. Version events  
7. Idempotent handlers  
8. Erasure tension  
9. When not to use  
10. Ledger vs source vs change-hear

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **append only**: compensate with new events  
२. **bound by aggregate**: not one global unordered log  
३. **project for reads**: do not scan the raw log on every page  
४. **snapshot for rebuild speed**: years of replay on boot fails  
५. **plan erasure early**: immutability meets forget-rights  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- edit past events in place  
- event source as default create-read-update-delete  
- no erasure story
