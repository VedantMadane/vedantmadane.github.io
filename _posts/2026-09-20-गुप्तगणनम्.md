### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **work inside a sealed seat**: open memory is a leak  
२. **demand outer attestation**: self-talk is not proof  
३. **seal memory**: do not print the seal in a log  
४. **keep authorization**: an enclave is not a god pass  
५. **keep seeds out of tickets**: a printed seed is a stolen seat  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- treat an enclave as enough authorization  
- skip attestation because the vendor said so  
- log sealing material
