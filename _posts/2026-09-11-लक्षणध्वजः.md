A **लक्षणध्वजः** is a short-lived switch that can hide a path without waiting for a deploy.

### Topics
1. No flag means a long fire
2. Kill without a deploy
3. Target not everyone
4. Kill stale flags
5. Method of a switch
6. Safe default
7. Owner and date
8. Flag is not a version
9. No secrets in the flag
10. Flag versus version
11. Five limbs
12. Close

<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **kill instantly with a flag**: do not wait for a deploy  
२. **target a cohort**: not the whole world first  
३. **default safe**: a dark flag must not open a red path  
४. **give every flag an owner and a death date**: stale flags are a second codebase  
५. **keep secrets out of flag payloads**: a public flag is a door  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- leave flags forever as architecture  
- default a new path on for everyone  
- store tokens in flag config
