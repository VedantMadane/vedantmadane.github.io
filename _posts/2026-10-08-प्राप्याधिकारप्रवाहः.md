<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **public clients use code plus proof**: no tokens in URL fragments  
२. **grant minimal scopes**: fat tokens magnify breach  
३. **short-lived access tokens**: rotate refresh and detect reuse  
४. **exact redirect allow-list**: state against login CSRF  
५. **validate issuer audience signature expiry**: never trust a parse  
६. **confidential secrets in vaults**: public clients have none  
७. **revoke paths and short TTL**: stolen tokens die quickly  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- implicit flow in fragments  
- star redirect lists  
- trust a token because it parses
