### What this post is about
**Inbox pattern** persists consumer-side message ids with business effects so at-least-once delivery does not double-apply work.

### Topics covered
1. Double delivery risk
2. Same transaction as side effects
3. Stable producer message ids
4. Durable unique constraint
5. Inbox row life
6. Reorder-tolerant handlers
7. Mark only with success
8. Pair with outbox
9. Duplicate hit metrics
10. Outbox vs inbox vs idempotency keys

### Sanskrit title
**अन्तःपेटिकाधर्मः** = *antaḥ-peṭikā-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the inner box" (inbox).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **insert inbox id with effects**: one local transaction  
२. **use producer message ids**: not fragile hashes only  
३. **unique constraint durable**: multi-node safe  
४. **archive inbox rows**: bound growth  
५. **tolerate reorder**: across partitions  
६. **never mark before success**: alone  
७. **pair outbox and inbox**: on money paths  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- check a memory set then act without a transaction  
- inbox insert before business commit in separate steps  
- unbounded inbox table forever
