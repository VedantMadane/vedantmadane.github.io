### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **bound every age**: no endless remember  
२. **invalidate on write**: TTL alone is slow  
३. **lock a stampede**: one fill not a flood  
४. **never cache private as public**: one user is not another  
५. **empty is a miss**: not a stored success  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- remember forever without a key plan  
- let every miss hit the origin at once  
- cache one user's answer as public
