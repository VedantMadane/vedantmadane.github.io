### What this post is about
Job-bound dharma is honest work quota. Measure job load, watch job fruit on a beat, stop when pace runs past the bound and never treat endless job as safe.

### Topics
1. Why bound the job
2. Measure job do not run endless
3. Method: hold bound measure watch
4. Watch job fruit
5. Stop excess pace
6. Write the job bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Release job when calm is proven
9. Not endless-job pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
कार्यसीमाधर्मः = dharma of job-bound (work quota).
