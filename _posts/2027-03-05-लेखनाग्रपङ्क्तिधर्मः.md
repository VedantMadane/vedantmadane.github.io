### What this post is about
The write-ahead line is durability before mutation. Dharma is log-first order, durable ink, ordered recovery and cleanup after success - never store-first hope.

### Topics
1. Why the ahead-line
2. Line then store
3. Method: write fill order
4. Durable ink
5. Recover by reading the line
6. Protect sequence
7. Clean after success
8. Name failure
9. Not reverse order
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
लेखनाग्रपङ्क्तिधर्मः = dharma of the write-ahead line (WAL).

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **write log before store mutation**: order  
२. **make the line durable before ack**: dur  
३. **recover by replaying the line**: replay  
४. **preserve log sequence**: seq  
५. **trim only after durable apply**: trim  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Mutate store then maybe log  
- Acknowledge before line is durable  
- Replay out of order  
- Delete log before apply confirmed  
- Silent log write failure
