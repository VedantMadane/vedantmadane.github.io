१. **अन्वयकुञ्चिका**: trace/request id end to end  
२. **मानकबन्धः**: W3C-class headers not snowflakes only

४. **बैगेज्सीमा**: baggage small and safe  
५. **गोप्यता**: no raw secrets in context  
६. **भङ्गदर्शनम्**: detect dropped context  
७. **पुस्तकसन्धिः**: libraries inject once not each app ad hoc

- new uuid every hop  
- PII in baggage  
- deadline reset at each service
