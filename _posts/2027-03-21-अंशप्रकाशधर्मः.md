### What this post is about
Partial-light dharma is honest feature exposure. Light a tiny share first, watch share fruit on a beat, expand only when proven and never let a share fault become full light.

### Topics
1. Why use partial light
2. Small share first never all
3. Method: hold lead watch
4. Watch share fruit
5. Kill share path on fault
6. Expand only when proven
7. Write the share bound first
8. Name the failure
9. Not full-light pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
अंशप्रकाशधर्मः = dharma of partial light (feature share).
