A shard key is destiny. Choose it for join and heat; changing it later is a migration war.

1. Why shard writes

7. Dual-write ban

10. Five limbs
11. Close

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **pick key for access pattern not fashion**: choice  
२. **detect and split hot keys**: heat  
३. **keep transactions inside one shard**: local  
४. **reshard with plan and backfill**: migrate  
५. **never dual-write old and new maps long**: ban  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- UUID-only keys forever hot  
- Cross-shard joins in hot path  
- Reshard by hope  
- Two routers both writable
