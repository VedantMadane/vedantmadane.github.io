### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **write for invariants**: commands  
२. **read for queries**: denormalized  
३. **declare lag SLO**: honest UX  
४. **projectors idempotent**: replay safe  
५. **do not split early**: complexity tax  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Two models no lag story  
- Dual write without outbox  
- Shared mutable tables both sides  
- CQRS for CRUD toy apps
