### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **issue from one guarded service**: not chat paste  
२. **short leases and rotate**: blast radius  
३. **dynamic DB or cloud creds**: no eternal passwords  
४. **audit every read and renew**: who saw  
५. **apps fetch at start not bake in image**: supply chain  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Secrets in git forever  
- No rotation path  
- Shared superuser key  
- No audit on read
