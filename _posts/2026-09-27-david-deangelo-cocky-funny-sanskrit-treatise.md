# विनोदप्रौढिपञ्चाशिका : चातुर्यविधिः
## *The Fifty Verses of Cocky & Funny: David DeAngelo's Double Your Dating in Classical Sanskrit Verse*

> **अभिज्ञानम् / Epigraph:**  
> *प्रौढस्य गर्वभावस्य हास्यस्य च समन्वयात् । विनोदप्रौढिरित्युक्ता कामवृद्धिकरी स्मृता ॥*  
> *"Through the harmonious fusion of playful arrogance and witty humor, Cocky and Funny is born; celebrated as the ultimate multiplier of romantic attraction."*

---

### प्रस्तावनारूपम् / Introduction
In 2001, **Eben Pagan**, writing under the pen name **David DeAngelo**, published *Double Your Dating*, introducing the revolutionary concept of **Cocky & Funny** (विनोदप्रौढिः). DeAngelo identified the fundamental error of modern men: the supplicating, permission-seeking "Nice Guy" paradigm that repels attractive women.

DeAngelo demonstrated that:
1. **Cocky alone** is arrogant, offensive and repels women through aggressive insecurity.
2. **Funny alone** turns a man into a harmless court jester or friendzoned comedian.
3. **Cocky & Funny combined** communicates high status, unshakeable confidence and emotional safety simultaneously.

This treatise: **विनोदप्रौढिपञ्चाशिका** codifies the complete philosophical and tactical system of Cocky & Funny into fifty classical Sanskrit verses (*padya*), verified against Pāṇinian metric rules (*Anuṣṭubh*). Every verse is accompanied by its word-by-word sandhi breakdown (*Padaccheda*), morphological grammatical tags, English translation strictly formatted without dashes or Oxford commas and actionable field commentary.
