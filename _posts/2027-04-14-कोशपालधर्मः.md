### What this post is about
Store-guard dharma is honest capacity custody. Guard store depth, watch store fruit on a beat, stop write when the store is full and never treat endless store as safe.

### Topics
1. Why guard the store
2. Guard store do not forget
3. Method: hold guard measure watch
4. Watch store fruit
5. Stop write when full
6. Write the store bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Release store when empty is proven
9. Not endless-store pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
कोशपालधर्मः = dharma of store-guard (capacity custody).
