<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
Route-guard dharma is honest path custody. Guard the route on every step, watch route fruit on a beat, stop traffic on a sick path and never treat a wrong road as the true way.

### Topics
1. Why guard the route
2. Guard route do not forget
3. Method: hold guard same watch
4. Watch route fruit
5. Stop the sick path
6. Write the route bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Lead path when route is proven
9. Not wrong-road pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
मार्गपालधर्मः = dharma of route-guard (path custody).
