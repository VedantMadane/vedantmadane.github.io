**Break-glass / JIT access** grants emergency privilege under ticket MFA time-box and heavy audit instead of permanent superuser standing access.

### Topics covered
1. Need a door in fire  
2. JIT over standing god  
3. MFA dual control step-up  
4. Hard auto expiry  
5. Least privilege scope  
6. Loud audit and pages  
7. Game-day rehearsal  
8. Catalog vendor glass too  
9. Post-use review  
10. Audit vs glass vs identity
