### What this post is about
**Long-lived connections** keep a bidirectional channel open across minutes or days and need explicit liveness auth backpressure and deploy manners.

### Topics covered
1. Silent death problem  
2. Bidirectional heartbeats  
3. Auth token lifetime  
4. Bounded backpressure  
5. Sticky or registry placement  
6. Scale via internal bus  
7. Versioned message schemas  
8. Conn and rate abuse caps  
9. Graceful close on drain  
10. SSE vs WebSocket vs pub/sub  

### Sanskrit title
**दीर्घसम्पर्कधर्मः** = *dīrgha-samparka-dharmaḥ* "dharma of long contact".

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **app-level heartbeat both ways**: detect zombies  
२. **refresh auth on long sockets**: no forever god ticket  
३. **bound per-socket buffers**: drop or disconnect  
४. **sticky or conn registry**: deliver pushes  
५. **scale with internal pub/sub**: to owning node  
६. **version wire schemas**: mobile stays up  
७. **drain with restartable close codes**: smooth reconnect  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- unbounded per-socket outbound queues  
- never heartbeat and trust TCP alone  
- deploy with kill -9 on full socket nodes
