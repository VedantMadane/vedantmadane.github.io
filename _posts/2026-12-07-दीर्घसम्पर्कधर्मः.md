### What this post is about
**दीर्घसम्पर्क** is a bidirectional channel that stays open across minutes or days and needs explicit liveness proof backpressure and deploy manners.

### Topics
1. Silence without a pulse  
2. Two-way heartbeat  
3. Refresh the entry symbol  
4. Bound the buffer  
5. Sticky or registered place  
6. Scale on an inner bus  
7. Version the wire form  
8. Cap threads and rate  
9. Drain with a sign  
10. One-way write vs two-way thread  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **app-level pulse both ways**: detect half-open death  
२. **refresh the entry symbol**: no standing god pass  
३. **bind per-thread buffers**: drop or disconnect  
४. **sticky session or contact register**: find the owner  
५. **scale with inner broadcast**: to the owning seat  
६. **version the wire form**: open clients survive  
७. **drain with a restartable close sign**: smooth reconnect  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- unbounded outbound lines per thread  
- trust the transport alone and never pulse  
- hard-kill a full socket node on deploy
