### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **degrade is a named mode**: design  
२. **errors stay visible to ops**: honest  
३. **users see reduced capability**: flag  
४. **unmute on measured recovery**: exit  
५. **never return 200 on failed write**: ban  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Swallow errors as success  
- No user banner in degrade  
- Mute forever without exit  
- Disable paging in mute
