### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **isolate stores by household**: no mixed rows  
२. **cap rate and bytes per tenant**: one cannot fill the field  
३. **split secrets**: one key is a blast  
४. **hear noisy neighbors**: do not stay mute  
५. **fair law**: not a favorite customer  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one shared key for every tenant  
- no per-tenant caps  
- treat a namespace label as isolation
