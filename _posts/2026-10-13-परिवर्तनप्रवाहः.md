<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **prefer log-based CDC**: triggers cost and miss  
२. **expand/contract column changes**: breaking shapes strand sinks  
३. **treat delivery as at-least-once**: idempotent sinks by key  
४. **do not double-publish the same fact**: outbox and CDC can both exist  
५. **snapshot then switch at a tracked position**: measure CDC lag  
६. **filter or tokenize sensitive columns**: per consumer need  
७. **propagate deletes on purpose**: tombstones consumers understand  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- poll full tables as the only change path  
- promise magic exactly-once everywhere  
- fan every column to every sink
