### What this post is about
**Backpressure** matches producer speed to consumer speed using bounds, credits, blocking or explicit rejection.

### Topics covered
1. Producer faster than consumer  
2. Bounded buffers  
3. Reject vs block vs silent drop  
4. Push vs pull  
5. Credit / reactive flow control  
6. Websocket buffer bounds  
7. Pool acquire timeouts  
8. Pressure metrics  
9. Sister practice: load shed  
10. Sister practice: rate limit  

### Sanskrit title
**प्रतिदाबधर्मः** = *pratidāba-dharmaḥ* "dharma of counter-pressure / backpressure".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **बद्धबफर्**: hard max depth  
२. **स्पष्टनिषेधः**: 503/slow-down not silent success  
३. **पुलप्राधान्यम्**: pull/credit when possible  
४. **dropमापनम्**: count drops if any  
५. **lagचेता**: consumer lag alerts  
६. **सूत्ररক্ষা**: timeouts on blocked acquires  
७. **प्राथमिकी**: what to slow first under pressure  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- unbounded in-memory queues as resilience  
- silent drop of user work  
- ignore consumer lag until OOM
