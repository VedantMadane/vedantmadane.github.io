<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **bound memory and disk buffers**: infinite buffer only delays crash  
२. **prefer explicit reject**: or controlled block  
३. **pull or credit where possible**: pure push needs another channel  
४. **honor credit end to end**: TCP and stream credits  
५. **bound per-connection write buffers**: drop slow clients by policy  
६. **metricize depth lag reject drop**: alert before memory death  
७. **use load shed at the door**: and pressure inside  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- unbounded in-memory queues  
- silent drop that looks like success  
- admission control as the only fix for a stuck consumer
