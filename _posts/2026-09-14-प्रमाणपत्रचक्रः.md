<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम्
१. **अल्पायुः**: short-lived certs where fit  
२. **स्वचालनवीकरणम्**: renew before expiry auto  
३. **श्रृङ्खलासत्यम्**: full chain served correctly  
४. **निरसनपथः**: revoke when compromised  
५. **अलार्मद्वयम्**: warn then critical windows  
६. **मालकः**: who owns each cert inventory  
७. **परीक्षासन्धिः**: staging renew drills  

### त्याज्यम्
- manual renew once a year only  
- ignore intermediate chain errors  
- private keys in images forever
