### What this post is about
**FinOps** brings near-real-time cloud cost visibility and accountability into engineering decisions alongside reliability.

### Topics covered
1. Invisible spend grows waste  
2. Cost per journey  
3. Tags and account allocation  
4. Rightsizing and off-hours  
5. Measured commit buys  
6. Egress and chatter in design  
7. Spend burn alerts  
8. Showback/chargeback  
9. Carbon with efficiency  
10. Error budget vs money vs scale dials  

### Sanskrit title
**वित्तोपस्करणधर्मः** = *vitta-upaskaraṇa-dharmaḥ* "dharma of finance-operations tooling" (FinOps).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

१. **unit cost per journey/product**: not only totals  
२. **enforce cost tags**: no mystery pile  
३. **rightsize and schedule idle off**: non-prod clocks  
४. **commit from baselines**: not blind RI  
५. **design review cost column**: egress storage chatty  
६. **alert on spend anomalies**: not month-end only  
७. **showback shared platforms**: kill free unlimited myth  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid

- keep non-prod at peak 24x7 year-round  
- discover cost only in finance month-close email
