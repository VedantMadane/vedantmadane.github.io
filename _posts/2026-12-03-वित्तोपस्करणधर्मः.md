### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **join spend to a unit of work**: not a lump bill  
२. **kill idle and night-always-on**: first cut  
३. **rightsize to the ask**: not fashion oversize  
४. **tag every billable with a human**: no orphans  
५. **show the team the unit bill weekly**: not month-three finance  
६. **commit the floor; spot the burst**: not all-in spot  
७. **page a budget breach**: not month-end grief  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- untagged leftover labs as the largest line  
- a single monthly PDF as the only cost signal  
- rightsizing by deleting the replica that holds the page
