### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **keep only for a named purpose**: not for greed  
२. **delete must be real**: not a flag  
३. **archive cold**: do not keep all hot  
४. **honor a legal hold**: without silent forever  
५. **test restore**: or the archive is theatre  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- keep everything forever just in case  
- soft-delete that never dies  
- mix audit rows into the same life as user fields
