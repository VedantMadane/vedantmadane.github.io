### What this post is about
**Geo and latency routing** steers clients toward a nearby healthy region under law and affinity constraints.

### Topics covered
1. Why near paths matter  
2. DNS anycast and latency maps  
3. Health over pure distance  
4. Session affinity choices  
5. Data residency law  
6. Failover drills  
7. Multi-writer split brain  
8. Edge cache vs stateful origin  
9. Per-region p99  
10. Links to multi-region policy and object stores  

### Sanskrit title
**भूस्थानमार्गणधर्मः** = *bhū-sthāna-mārgaṇa-dharmaḥ* "dharma of place-based routing".

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **health before distance**: always  
२. **document affinity**: sticky or free hop  
३. **encode residency**: before latency map  
४. **drill region loss**: real TTLs  
५. **one writer story**: or explicit CRDT  
६. **split read/write paths**: edge vs origin  
७. **SLO per region**: not global mean only  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- route to nearest regardless of health  
- untested DNS failover only on paper  
- dual primary writes with no conflict plan
