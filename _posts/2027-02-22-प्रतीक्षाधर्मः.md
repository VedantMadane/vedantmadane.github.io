### What this post is about
A wait without a bound is a slow death. Write the limit, share it with both sides, cancel past the limit and climb back without synchronized storms.

### Topics
1. Why bound the wait
2. Two clocks meet
3. Write the limit early
4. Cancel past the limit
5. Client knows the bound
6. Nested waits
7. Count time on retry
8. Jitter restart
9. Not hope-waiting
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **publish timeout on both sides**: two clocks  
२. **cancel work after deadline**: cancel  
३. **inner deadline shorter than outer**: nested  
४. **jitter restarts after timeout**: jitter  
५. **never wait on hope alone**: hope  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Infinite client wait with no server deadline  
- Keep running work after the deadline silently  
- Outer timeout shorter than inner call chain  
- All clients retry at the same instant
