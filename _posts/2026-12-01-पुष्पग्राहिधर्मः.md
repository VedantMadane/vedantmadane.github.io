### What this post is about
**Bloom filters** are probabilistic set sketches that never false-negative on membership when correct and may false-positive within a designed rate.

### Topics covered
1. Tiny maybe-present memory  
2. FP ok FN forbidden  
3. Size from n and p  
4. Classic no-delete  
5. Hash independence  
6. Cache penetration fit  
7. Versioned multi-node filters  
8. Monitor real FP  
9. Cuckoo and friends  
10. Cache vs Bloom vs index  

### Sanskrit title
**पुष्पग्राहिधर्मः** = *puṣpa-grāhi-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the flower-catcher" (Bloom).

### English

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **treat maybe as confirm-needed**: never as no  
२. **size bits from n and target FP**: do not overfill  
३. **plan deletes via counting/rebuild**: classic cannot forget  
४. **use strong independent hashes**: uniform bits  
५. **fit negative-lookup paths**: not authz  
६. **version filters across nodes**: same epoch  
७. **monitor observed FP rate**: in prod  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- use Bloom alone to authorize access  
- overfill a tiny filter and ignore FP explosion  
- delete keys from a classic Bloom in place
