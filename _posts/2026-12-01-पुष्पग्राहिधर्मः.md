<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**पुष्पग्राहि** is a probabilistic membership sketch. It can say a key may be present when it is not. It must not say a key is absent when it was inserted.

### Topics
1. Thin membership sketch  
2. Bit-row and digests  
3. True miss versus maybe-yes  
4. Fill ratio  
5. Method of measure  
6. No naive delete  
7. Union not difference  
8. Cache gate not money write  
9. Exact store still required  
10. Cache versus bloom versus digest  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **use as a negative sieve**: never as the fact store  
२. **size bits and digest count**: watch fill  
३. **treat a zero bit as truth**: treat all-ones as suspicion  
४. **do not clear bits to delete**: unless counting  
५. **or-merge equal shapes only**: no honest minus  
६. **keep an exact list for money and uniqueness**: bloom in front  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- authorize a payment on a bloom yes  
- delete from a plain bloom by zeroing bits  
- let fill climb until every probe is yes
