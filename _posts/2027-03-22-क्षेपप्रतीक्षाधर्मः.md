### What this post is about
Throw-wait dharma is honest flow control. When the stream fills wait before throwing more, keep pace measured, watch flood marks on a beat and never treat endless pace as safety.

### Topics
1. Why wait before throw
2. Keep pace measured
3. Method: wait measure watch
4. Watch the flood mark
5. Stop pace when stream is full
6. Write the pace bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Free pace only when empty is proven
9. Not endless-pace pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
क्षेपप्रतीक्षाधर्मः = dharma of throw-wait (backpressure).
