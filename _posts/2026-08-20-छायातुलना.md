<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम्
१. **छायाप्रतिलिपिः**: copy requests to candidate  
२. **अदहनम्**: shadow answers not returned to user  
३. **विभेदलेखः**: diff status latency body hashes  
४. **नमुना**: sample rate under cost budget  
५. **उत्पादनसदृशम्**: real prod shape not synthetic only  
६. **गोप्यता**: PII handling same as prod path  
७. **सीमासन्धिः**: shadow must not sink primary  

### त्याज्यम्
- shadow that writes side effects to prod data  
- 100 percent mirror without capacity  
- no diffs collected so shadow is theater
