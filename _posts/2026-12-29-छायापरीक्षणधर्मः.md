१. **shadow never mutates prod data**: safety  
२. **compare on contract fields**: not raw noise  
३. **cap shadow QPS and spend**: bound  
४. **promote only on scored diffs**: criteria  
५. **redact PII in shadow logs**: privacy

- Unbounded fanout cost  
- Compare everything bytewise forever  
- Shadow logs full PAN
