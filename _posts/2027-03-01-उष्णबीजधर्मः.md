### What this post is about
A hot key is a seed of traffic that burns a shared path. Dharma is isolation, bounded heat queues and continuous watch.

### Topics
1. Why hot seeds burn
2. Know heat then split
3. Method: isolate bound watch
4. Bound the hot queue
5. Measured hot depth
6. Keep cold off the hot path
7. Watch on a clock
8. Drill isolation
9. Not average-blindness
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
उष्णबीजधर्मः = dharma of the hot seed (hot key).
