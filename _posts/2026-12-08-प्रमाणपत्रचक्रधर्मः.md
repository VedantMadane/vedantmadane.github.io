### What this post is about
**Certificate lifecycle** is the operational path to issue renew monitor and revoke certificates before trust expires or leaks.

### Topics covered
1. Expiry as outage class  
2. Owned inventory  
3. Auto renew with failure pages  
4. Short lifetimes  
5. CT logs and external probes  
6. Public vs private CA tiers  
7. Revoke on leak  
8. KMS/HSM custody  
9. Alert and deploy gates  
10. mTLS vs lifecycle vs keys  

### Sanskrit title
**प्रमाणपत्रचक्रधर्मः** = *pramāṇapatra-cakra-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the certificate cycle".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **inventory certs with owners+expiry**: no orphans  
२. **auto renew well before notAfter**: page issuance fail  
३. **prefer shorter lifetimes**: force path health  
४. **watch CT and external HTTPS**: not only internal sheet  
५. **separate public and private CAs**: policy split  
६. **practice revoke+reissue**: on leak drills  
७. **block deploys shipping soon-dead edge certs**: gates  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- manual renew the week of expiry as the only process  
- PEM private keys in chat tickets  
- no owner on the wildcard that fronts checkout
