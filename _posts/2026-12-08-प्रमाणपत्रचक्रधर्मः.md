### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **inventory papers with owners and end-times**: no orphans  
२. **auto renew well before the end**: page issuance fail  
३. **prefer shorter lives**: force path health  
४. **watch public logs and outer probes**: not only an inner sheet  
५. **split public and private issuers**: policy split  
६. **practice revoke and reissue**: on leak drills  
७. **block deploys shipping soon-dead edge papers**: gates  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- manual renew the week of expiry as the only process  
- private keys riding in chat tickets  
- no owner on the wildcard that fronts checkout
