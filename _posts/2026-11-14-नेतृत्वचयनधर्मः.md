### What this post is about
**Leader election** selects one instance to own a singleton duty using time-bounded leases and fencing so two actives do not corrupt shared work.

### Topics covered
1. Singleton collision problem  
2. Lease and heartbeat  
3. Fencing tokens  
4. Fast leadership loss detection  
5. Duties that fit election  
6. Graceful step-down  
7. Quorum lease stores  
8. Transition metrics  
9. Partition minority stop  
10. Shard multi-leader vs one leader  

### Sanskrit title
**नेतृत्वचयनधर्मः** = *netṛtva-cayana-dharmaḥ* "dharma of choosing leadership".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **TTL lease + heartbeat**: no infinite lock  
२. **fencing token on writes**: reject stale leader  
३. **fast watch on loss**: short dual window  
४. **elect only singletons**: not per-request  
५. **resign on graceful stop**: skip full TTL wait  
६. **quorum-backed lease store**: cluster visible  
७. **minority stops on partition**: no split brain act  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- two schedulers without lease  
- accept writes from old leader after re-elect  
- local file as cluster leadership truth
