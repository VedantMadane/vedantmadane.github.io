### What this post is about
**Graceful shutdown** is the ordered exit path that stops new work, drains in-flight requests and hands off jobs before process death.

### Topics covered
1. Why sudden death hurts
2. Ordered terminate handling
3. Readiness before liveness kill
4. Bounded drain and cancel
5. Edge and mesh drain
6. Worker checkpoint or nack
7. Stateful flush
8. Hook vs grace alignment
9. In-flight drain metrics
10. Shed vs shutdown vs journal

### Sanskrit title
**अनुग्रहनिरोधधर्मः** = *anugraha-nirodha-dharmaḥ* "dharma of gracious stopping".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **not-ready first**: then close listen  
२. **bound drain time**: then cancel clear  
३. **coordinate edge drain**: with the app  
४. **nack/checkpoint jobs**: no ack-then-die  
५. **flush stateful journals**: in grace  
६. **align hooks and grace**: with slack  
७. **metric inflight during drain**: prove safety  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- kill ready instances without draining  
- ack queue messages then exit immediately  
- hook longer than the grace period always
