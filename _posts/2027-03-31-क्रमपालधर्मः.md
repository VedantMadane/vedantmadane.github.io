### What this post is about
Sequence-guard dharma is honest write order. Hold even sequence, watch sequence fruit on a beat, stop write on uneven order and never treat disorder as safe lead.

### Topics
1. Why guard sequence
2. Protect order do not forget
3. Method: hold even watch
4. Watch sequence fruit
5. Stop write on uneven order
6. Write the sequence bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Lead write only on proven order
9. Not disorder pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
क्रमपालधर्मः = dharma of sequence guard.
