**Consumer lag is how far behind the head a consumer group is in time or offset. Growing lag is operational debt not a free buffer forever.**

### Topics covered
1. Why lag is debt  
2. Measure clearly  
3. Age over count  
4. Hot-slice skew  
5. Scale both sides  
6. Backpressure  
7. Unstick poison  
8. Stable membership  
9. Alert on age and growth  
10. Fan-out vs queue vs change-hear
