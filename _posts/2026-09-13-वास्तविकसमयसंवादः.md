### ग्राह्यम्
१. **हृदयस्पन्दः**: liveness of idle connections  
२. **पुनःसन्धिनीतिः**: client reconnect with jitter  
३. **पृष्ठताबन्धः**: server outbound buffer limits  
४. **अधिकारः**: auth on connect and refresh  
५. **क्षैतिजसत्यम्**: sticky or shared pubsub known  
६. **निष्क्रयकाले द्रवः**: drain sockets on deploy  
७. **मापनम्**: connects messages drops  

### त्याज्यम्
- infinite connections per IP silently  
- no heartbeat zombie sockets  
- broadcast without backpressure
