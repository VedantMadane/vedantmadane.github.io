3. Guard order

7. Once-key on reconnect
8. Keep secrets off the wire log
9. One-way is not two-way
10. Stream versus speech
11. Five limbs
12. Close

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **keep message order**: a swapped pair breaks the story  
२. **tell presence truthfully**: do not mark the dead as live  
३. **bound fan-out**: one box must not hold every thread  
४. **once-key on reconnect**: do not replay a fruit  
५. **keep secrets off the public wire log**: a chat log is a leak  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- treat a long socket as enough dharma  
- mark everyone live forever  
- fan every event to every box
