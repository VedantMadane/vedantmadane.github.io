### What this post is about
**Sharding** partitions a dataset across multiple stores so storage and write throughput scale beyond one primary. It is not read-replica scale and it is not a consistent-digest placement trick alone.

### Topics
1. Horizontal write scale  
2. Portion-key fate  
3. Range digest directory  
4. Avoid cross-portion binds  
5. Single-portion method  
6. Reshard from day one  
7. Secondary index pain  
8. Locality-friendly keys  
9. Routing maps  
10. Replica versus portion versus digest place  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya. Verses say अंश not the loan शार्ड.

१. **choose keys against skew**: and locality  
२. **keep common binds single-portion**: avoid scatter tax

४. **prefer local indexes**: careful globals  
५. **central routing map**: no app hardcode  
६. **alert per portion**: not fleet mean only  
७. **entity or tenant keys**: often beat pure random  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid

- scatter-gather every user click  
- no reshard story with a fixed count forever
