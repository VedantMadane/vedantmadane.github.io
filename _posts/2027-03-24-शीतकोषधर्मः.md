### What this post is about
Cold-store dharma is honest long custody of old facts. Lead stale writing into cold, never mix cold with hot paths, watch cold fruit on a beat and destroy what has gone past life.

### Topics
1. Why hold a cold store
2. Lead old writing into cold not hot
3. Method: hold lead watch
4. Watch cold fruit
5. Burn stale cold on a beat
6. Write the store bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Never mix cold with hot path
9. Not hot-store-for-old pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
शीतकोषधर्मः = dharma of the cold store (archival).
