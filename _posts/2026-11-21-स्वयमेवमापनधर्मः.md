### What this post is about
**Autoscaling** changes replica count from operational signals so capacity tracks demand within safe bounds.

### Topics covered
1. Elastic vs fixed fleet
2. Signals beyond host load
3. Warmup and readiness
4. Fast out slow in
5. Min availability floor
6. Max cost and dependency ceiling
7. Schedule plus reactive
8. Scoped tenant fairness
9. Drain on scale-in
10. Shed budget and scale roles

### Sanskrit title
**स्वयमेवमापनधर्मः** = *svayameva-māpana-dharmaḥ* "dharma of measuring/scaling by itself".

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **scale on lag/pain/concurrency**: not host load only  
२. **warmup before ready**: no cold join peak  
३. **fast out slow in**: hysteresis  
४. **min floor critical paths**: product choice on zero  
५. **max ceiling**: cost and dep safety  
६. **pre-warm known peaks**: plus reactive  
७. **drain on scale-in**: graceful path  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- host-load-only scale for queue-bound workers  
- scale-in by hard kill of ready instances  
- no max replica cap on shared store backends
