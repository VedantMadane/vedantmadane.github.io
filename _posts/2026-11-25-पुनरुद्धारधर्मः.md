### What this post is about
**Backup and restore** preserve recoverable copies of state with explicit loss and downtime objectives proven by drills. A green copy job is not a restore.

### Topics
1. Restore is the product  
2. Recovery-point and recovery-time numbers  
3. Full incremental snapshot logical mix  
4. Immutable off-account copies  
5. Calendar restore drills  
6. Encrypt with separable keys  
7. App versus crash consistency  
8. Multi-store catalog  
9. Detect time inside recovery time  
10. Replica versus backup versus geo  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya. No Latin short forms in verses.

१. **publish numeric loss and dark-time targets**: with owners  
२. **immutable off-account copies**: ransomware distance  
३. **restore drills on a calendar**: measure real dark time  
४. **encrypt backups**: keys recoverable apart  
५. **know consistency class**: app versus crash  
६. **catalog all stores**: not the database only  
७. **include detect and page time**: in recovery-time math

- only same-account snapshots for a ransomware plan
