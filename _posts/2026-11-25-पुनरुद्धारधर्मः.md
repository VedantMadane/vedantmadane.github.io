### What this post is about
**Backup and restore** preserve recoverable copies of state with explicit loss and downtime objectives proven by drills.

### Topics covered
1. Restore is the product  
2. RPO and RTO numbers  
3. Full incr snapshot logical mix  
4. Immutable off-account copies  
5. Calendar restore drills  
6. Encrypt with separable keys  
7. App vs crash consistency  
8. Multi-store catalog  
9. Detect time inside RTO  
10. Replica vs backup vs geo  

### Sanskrit title
**पुनरुद्धारधर्मः** = *punar-uddhāra-dharmaḥ* "dharma of lifting up again" (restore).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **publish numeric RPO/RTO**: with owners  
२. **immutable off-account copies**: ransomware distance  
३. **restore drills on calendar**: measure real RTO  
४. **encrypt backups**: keys recoverable apart  
५. **know consistency class**: app vs crash  
६. **catalog all stores**: not DB only  
७. **include detect+page time**: in RTO math  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- declare RTO without ever restoring  
- only same-account snapshots for ransomware plan  
- green backup job as sole success metric
