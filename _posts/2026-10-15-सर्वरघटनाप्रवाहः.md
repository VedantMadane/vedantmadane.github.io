<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **speak the SSE field format**: event data id retry  
२. **event ids let resume after drop**: Last-Event-ID  
३. **periodic comment heartbeats**: idle middles must not close  
४. **authenticate the subscribe**: drop when the session expires  
५. **bound server buffers**: disconnect slow readers  
६. **sticky route or shared pub/sub**: multi-instance fanout  
७. **disable proxy buffering on the path**: or events arrive late in bursts  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- use full duplex for notify-only faces  
- skip event ids  
- leave proxy buffering on
