### What this post is about
Entry-stop dharma is honest admission at the gate. Cap who may enter when the door fills, watch gate fruit on a beat, refuse new entry under flood and open again only after empty proof.

### Topics
1. Why stop entry at the gate
2. Keep entry measured
3. Method: stop measure watch
4. Watch gate fruit
5. Stop entry when gate is full
6. Write the entry bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Free entry only when empty is proven
9. Not endless-entry pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
प्रवेशरोधधर्मः = dharma of entry-stop (admission control).
