<p class="verse-topic">पद्ययुग्मम् 28 (श्लोकाः 55–56)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v28">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="648.0" data-end="654.0">विभक्तेषु गृहेष्वेव चरन्नाग्रहवान् भवेत्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="654.0" data-end="660.0">गुटात् महत्तरं सर्वं न केवलं तु विजयम्॥५५॥</span><br />
  <span data-line="3" data-start="660.0" data-end="666.0">न्यायकर्तव्यसम्बन्धे वादयोग्यां च भारतीम्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="4" data-start="666.0" data-end="672.0">अवशेषयति यः सोऽयं सभ्यताया हि दीपकः॥५६॥</span>
</div>

<p class="prose-text">Moving among divided houses, insistent—the whole larger than any faction, not victory alone—he leaves speech for arguing justice, duty and belonging: civilization's lamp.</p>

<details>
<summary>अन्वयः — संस्कृतार्थः</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

**अन्वयः / सारार्थः**: विभक्तेषु गृहेषु चरन् आग्रहवान्—गुटात् महत्तरं सर्वं, न विजयमात्रम्। न्यायकर्तव्यसम्बन्धे वादयोग्यां भारतीं अवशेषयति—स सभ्यतादीपकः।

</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>व्याकरणटिप्पणी (संस्कारबीजम्)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

</div>
</details>

  <span data-line="1" data-start="672.0" data-end="678.0">भारतीयस्वबोधस्य दीर्घाभिलेखसञ्चये।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="678.0" data-end="684.0">राजनैतिकचमत्कारः कः स्यादिति विचार्यताम्॥५७॥</span><br />
  <span data-line="3" data-start="684.0" data-end="690.0">न यत् पूजित एवासौ देवत्वेन जगत्त्रये।</span><br />
  <span data-line="4" data-start="690.0" data-end="696.0">यद् भिन्ना जनाः सर्वे एककथाभागिनः कृताः॥५८॥</span>

<p class="prose-text">In India's long archive of self-knowledge, what is the political miracle? Not that he was worshipped as god—but that diverse peoples were made co-sharers of one story.</p>

</div>
</details>
