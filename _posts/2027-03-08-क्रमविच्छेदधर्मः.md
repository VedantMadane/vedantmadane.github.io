### What this post is about
A sequence-cut stops calling a sick dependency. Dharma is close on fault growth, half-open probe, timed reopen and honest signals.

### Topics
1. Why cut the sequence
2. Close on fault growth
3. Method: close open measure
4. Timed not eternal cut
5. Probe while closed
6. Cut high first
7. Signal on block
8. Name failure
9. Not eternal open
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
क्रमविच्छेदधर्मः = dharma of the sequence-cut (circuit breaker).
