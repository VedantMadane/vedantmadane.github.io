### What this post is about
**Software supply-chain safety** makes build inputs known reproducible signed and continuously scanned before they become production.

### Topics covered
1. Unknown boxes problem  
2. SBOM inventories  
3. Pins and lockfiles  
4. Sign and verify on deploy  
5. Build provenance  
6. Continuous CVE scan gates  
7. Owned slim base images  
8. Least-privilege CI tokens  
9. Transitive CVE playbooks  
10. Egress vs supply vs keys  

### Sanskrit title
**आपूर्तिक्षेमधर्मः** = *āpūrti-kṣema-dharmaḥ* "dharma of supply safety".

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **emit SBOM every release**: image and app  
२. **commit lockfiles and digests**: no floating latest  
३. **sign artifacts**: verify in deploy admit  
४. **attest build provenance**: source and builder  
५. **scan continuous**: gate critical CVE  
६. **own slim base images**: patch yourself  
७. **scope CI tokens short-lived**: isolate sign keys  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- deploy unsigned latest from public hub  
- no SBOM and week-long CVE inventory guesses  
- CI god-token that pushes prod and signs
