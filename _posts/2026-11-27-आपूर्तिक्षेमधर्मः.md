### What this post is about
**Software supply-chain safety** makes build inputs known reproducible signed and continuously scanned before they become production. Egress guards what leaves. This post guards what enters.

### Topics
1. Unknown boxes problem  
2. Bill-of-materials inventories  
3. Pins and lockfiles  
4. Sign and verify on deploy  
5. Build provenance  
6. Continuous defect scan gates  
7. Owned slim base images  
8. Least-privilege build tokens  
9. Transitive defect playbooks  
10. Egress versus supply versus keys  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **emit a bill of materials every release**: image and app  
२. **commit lockfiles and digests**: no floating latest  
३. **sign artifacts**: verify in deploy admit  
४. **attest build provenance**: source and builder  
५. **scan continuously**: gate critical defects  
६. **own slim base images**: patch yourself  
७. **scope build tokens short-lived**: isolate sign keys  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- deploy unsigned latest from a public hub  
- no inventory and week-long defect guesses  
- a god-token that pushes prod and signs

| संस्कृतपदम् | Word-for-word English |
