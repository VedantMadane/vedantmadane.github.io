### What this post is about
Hot-bound dharma is honest rate control. Cap hot traffic before it burns the path, watch heat fruit on a beat, stop when the bound is crossed and free heat only after calm proof.

### Topics
1. Why bound the heat
2. Keep heat measured
3. Method: bound measure watch
4. Watch heat fruit
5. Stop when heat is over
6. Write the heat bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Free heat only when calm is proven
9. Not endless-heat pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
उष्णसीमाधर्मः = dharma of the hot bound (rate limit).
