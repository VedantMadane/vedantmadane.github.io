<details>
<summary>English · overview and topics (minimizable)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Shadow traffic** mirrors live requests onto a candidate implementation while users still receive only the primary response.

### Topics
1. Why copy live traffic
2. Never return shadow to client
3. Diff status latency bodies
4. Sample and capacity budget
5. Ban unsafe side effects
6. Authz and secret parity
7. Idempotency if any write
8. Flag kill switch
9. Dark launch then small slice
10. Contrast with canary and blue-green
11. Five limbs
12. Close

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.
