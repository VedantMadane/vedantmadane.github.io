### What this post is about
This is one full synthesis of Owen Cooks teaching arc (Blueprint Decoded through later presence and polarity work) cast as classical padya. The English names the modern source; the Sanskrit holds the pure path.

Owen Cooks through-line is simple and hard: you do not do attractiveness; you become the man whose presence already is attractive. Core confidence beats situational confidence. State rises from self-assessment not from the room. Outcome independence keeps others from writing your value. Masculine polarity is action from own intention not reaction to her face. Authenticity and presence replace scripts. You attract what you are.

### Teaching map (twelve limbs)
1. Essence first: attraction is being
2. Core confidence over mere situation
3. Method: hold essence intention presence
4. State from self not from other
5. Outcome independence
6. Write authenticity bound first
7. Name the failure honestly
8. Lead path when own intention is proven
9. Not others-praise as dharma
10. Five limbs sealed
11. Close with own-intention essence
12. Final seal

### Sanskrit title
पुरुषसारधर्मः = dharma of masculine essence (full Owen Cook synthesis).

### Source frame (English only)
Owen Cook known as RSD Tyler taught Real Social Dynamics for years: Blueprint Decoded core confidence and self-assessment; high-value communication; masculine polarity as act through your own intentions; outcome independence; authenticity over social conditioning; presence and state; you attract what you are. This post does not teach pickup lines. It holds the inner spine of that teaching in pure Devanagari padya.
