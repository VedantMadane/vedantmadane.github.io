<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **configure core max and bounded queue**: explicit reject when full  
२. **separate pools for blocking IO and CPU**: slow disk must not starve compute  
३. **fail the caller clearly**: or block with timeout  
४. **propagate cancellation**: abandoned work must not burn threads  
५. **export pool gauges and rejects**: capacity planning needs them  
६. **avoid same-pool re-entrant waits**: self-deadlock  
७. **align thread max with store pool size**: do not stampede a smaller pool  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- unbounded thread-per-request  
- silent discard when full  
- pretty async that spawns unbounded tasks
