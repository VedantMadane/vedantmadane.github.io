### What this post is about
A bulkhead is a room with its own wall. Dharma is separate pools, written capacity, measured fence and fault containment.

### Topics
1. Why measure-fence
2. Separate rooms
3. Method: split bound fence
4. Room bounds
5. Contain fault
6. Watch load
7. Measured fence
8. Name failure
9. Not mixed pit
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
परिमाणरोधधर्मः = dharma of the measure-fence (bulkhead).

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **isolate pools by domain**: split  
२. **publish per-room bounds**: bound  
३. **contain fault in one room**: contain  
४. **watch each room load**: watch  
५. **fence a room without global kill**: fence  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- One shared pool for all work  
- No per-pool capacity limit  
- Cascade one slow dependency everywhere  
- No room-level metrics  
- Global kill as first response
