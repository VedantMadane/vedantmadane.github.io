२. **publish contracts**: no silent table join  
३. **measure cross-call cost**: chatty tax  
४. **deploy independently**: real boundary  
५. **keep data with writer service**: ownership  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Nano-services for every noun  
- Shared mutable DB as integration  
- Distributed monolith  
- No owner on the edge
