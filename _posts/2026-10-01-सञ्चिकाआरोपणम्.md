### What this post is about
**File upload** endpoints accept untrusted bytes. They need hard limits, type checks, safe storage and often async scanning.

### Topics covered
1. Unbounded upload risk  
2. Size and allow-list types at the door  
3. Do not trust extensions  
4. Store outside web root / buckets  
5. Presigned direct upload  
6. Multipart resume + idempotency  
7. Malware quarantine  
8. Signed URL privacy  
9. Async transcode  
10. Rate limits and tenant quotas  

### Sanskrit title
**सञ्चिकाआरोपणम्** = *sañcikā-āropaṇam* "mounting/uploading of files".

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **आकारद्वारम्**: max bytes early  
२. **allowlist प्रकार**: not blocklist only  
३. **सर्वरनाम**: random object keys  
४. **पृथग्भण्डारः**: no web-root exec  
५. **मैलक्वारन्टीन**: scan before publish  
६. **अंशकुञ्चिका**: resumable upload id  
७. **कुटुम्बकोटा**: per-tenant bytes  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- trust Content-Type alone  
- serve uploads as executable templates  
- unlimited size on multiparts
