**File upload** endpoints accept untrusted bytes. They need hard limits, type checks, safe storage and often async scanning.

1. Unbounded upload risk

5. Signed direct upload
6. Chunked resume
7. Quarantine before publish
8. Signed path privacy

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **size cap at the door**: before buffering  
२. **allow-list types**: not blocklist only  
३. **server-chosen names**: never client filenames  
४. **store outside web root**: never execute blobs  
५. **scan before publish**: quarantine when needed  
६. **resume id for chunks**: no silent duplicates  
७. **per-tenant byte bounds**: one customer cannot fill storage  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- trust Content-Type or suffixes alone  
- serve uploads as executable templates  
- unlimited multipart size
