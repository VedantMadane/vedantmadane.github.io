<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Transactional notifications** (OTP, receipts, incident mail) need a delivery system with retries, bounce handling and privacy not only a `send()` call.

### Topics covered
1. Email vs SMS channel traits  
2. Idempotent provider calls  
3. Soft/hard bounce and complaints  
4. Signed status webhooks  
5. Template versioning and injection safety  
6. OTP secrecy in logs  
7. Send rate and reputation  
8. Queue-based send path  
9. Vendor risk and fallbacks  

### Sanskrit title
**सन्देशवितरणम्** = *sandeśa-vitaraṇam* "dispatching of messages".

१. **इदमर्थप्रेषणम्**: idempotency key per logical notify  
२. **प्रतिक्षेपबुद्धिः**: bounce class handling

४. **टेम्प्लेट्कोशः**: versioned templates  
५. **OTPगोप्यता**: no OTP in logs  
६. **पङ्क्तिपथः**: async workers  
७. **कीर्तिरक्षा**: reputation-aware rates  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- SMTP on request thread  
- retry hard bounces forever  
- log OTPs in plain application logs
