**लेखापरीक्षा** persists a queryable change-resistant record of security-relevant acts for reconstruction after disputes breaches and reviews.

### Topics
1. Truth after the fact  
2. Structured who-what-when  
3. Join-only integrity  
4. Separate from debug  
5. Denies and emergency opens  
6. Clocks and marks  
7. Privileged read of audit  
8. Class-based retain  
9. Pipeline lag alerts  
10. Redaction vs audit vs event source  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **structured actor/act/object/time/result**: always  
२. **join-only or hash-chain store**: no silent edit  
३. **separate sink from debug**: different retain  
४. **log denies and admin breaks**: not only success  
५. **sync clocks and unique marks**: merge honest  
६. **audit the auditors**: read is sensitive  
७. **page on audit path lag or drop**: trail health  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- editable shared sheet as the audit of record  
- only log successful admin acts  
- mix short debug with long audit in one index
