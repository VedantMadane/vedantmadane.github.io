### What this post is about
**Audit logging** persists a queryable tamper-resistant record of security-relevant actions for reconstruction after disputes breaches and reviews.

### Topics covered
1. Truth after the fact  
2. Structured who-what-when  
3. Append-only integrity  
4. Separate from debug  
5. Denies and break-glass  
6. Clocks and event ids  
7. Privileged read of audit  
8. Class-based retention  
9. Pipeline lag alerts  
10. Redaction vs audit vs event source  

### Sanskrit title
**लेखापरीक्षाधर्मः** = *lekhā-parīkṣā-dharmaḥ* "dharma of examining the written account".

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **structured actor/action/object/time/result**: always  
२. **append-only or hash-chain store**: no silent edit  
३. **separate sink/ACL from debug**: different retain  
४. **log denies and admin breaks**: not only success  
५. **sync clocks + unique event ids**: merge honest  
६. **audit the auditors**: read is sensitive  
७. **page on audit pipeline lag/drop**: trail SLO  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- editable shared spreadsheet as audit of record  
- only log successful admin actions  
- mix 7-day debug with 7-year audit in one index
