### What this post is about
**A write-ahead log records intended changes before applying them to the durable state so crash recovery can finish or undo work safely.**

### Topics covered
1. Why write first  
2. Intent before mutate  
3. Durable barrier  
4. Group commit  
5. Checkpoint and truncate  
6. Integrity  
7. Redo or undo  
8. Replica order  
9. Outbox is another layer  
10. Source vs engine vs birth-box  

### Sanskrit title
**लेखनाग्रधर्मः**.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **log intent first**: then mutate  
२. **durable barrier**: not page-cache success  
३. **group commit**: when delay allows  
४. **checkpoint and truncate**: unbounded log slows recovery  
५. **checksum torn tails**: do not apply silence  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- mutate-first then hope  
- treat a buffer write as durable  
- confuse app outbox with engine log
