<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
Load-balance dharma is honest even share of work. Even the load across sides, watch load fruit on a beat, stop the path when load is uneven and never treat uneven load as settled dharma.

### Topics
1. Why even the load
2. Make load even not uneven
3. Method: hold balance even watch
4. Watch load fruit
5. Stop uneven path
6. Write the load bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Lead load when even is proven
9. Not uneven-load pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
भारसमधर्मः = dharma of load-balance (even load share).
