### What this post is about
A **distributed lock** coordinates mutual exclusion across processes. Done wrong it deadlocks clusters or double-executes work.

### Topics covered
1. Why cluster mutual exclusion  
2. Lease/TTL  
3. Fencing tokens  
4. Jittered acquire  
5. High contention redesign  
6. Release discipline  
7. Lock store failure modes  
8. Key naming and tenancy  
9. Metrics  
10. Contrast with consensus leaders  

### Sanskrit title
**वितरिततालः** = *vitarita-tālaḥ* "distributed latch/lock".

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **कालबद्धlease**: TTL on every hold  
२. **वर्धमानटोकन्**: fencing on writes  
३. **विस्पन्दग्रहणम्**: backoff jitter  
४. **संसाधननाम**: fine-grained keys  
५. **मुक्तिपथः**: finally unlock  
६. **मापनम्**: wait/hold metrics  
७. **अल्पखण्डः**: short critical sections  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- lock without TTL  
- one global lock for all work  
- treat single-node lock as consensus
