### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **separate pools by criticality**: split  
२. **bound queues per cell**: bound  
३. **fail closed loud not silent wait**: reject  
४. **no shared critical thread pool**: share  
५. **drill neighbor failure monthly**: drill  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- One pool for all tenants  
- Unbounded queues per service  
- Block forever on slow dependency  
- No blast-radius map
