<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **design from slow queries**: every index slows writes  
२. **honor leftmost composite order**: do not guess  
३. **prefer fewer hot-path indexes**: budget storage and writes  
४. **document partial and covering**: nobody drops them as unused  
५. **check stats before drop**: monthly close may still need it  
६. **index join and cascade keys**: unindexed keys hurt deletes  
७. **build online off peak**: watch lock and replica lag  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- guess indexes without plans  
- unlimited indexes on every column  
- drop a rare index that guards a close path
