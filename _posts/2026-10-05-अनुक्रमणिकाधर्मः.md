### What this post is about
**Indexes** speed selective reads and enforce some constraints. They cost storage and slow writes so they must match real queries.

### Topics covered
1. Table scans vs indexed access  
2. Explain-driven creation  
3. Composite leftmost order  
4. Too many indexes  
5. Partial and covering indexes  
6. Dropping safely  
7. Selectivity  
8. Online/concurrent builds  
9. Foreign-key companion indexes  
10. Links to pagination and time-series access  

### Sanskrit title
**अनुक्रमणिकाधर्मः** = *anukramaṇikā-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the index".

- drop unused without checking monthly jobs  
- build huge indexes on primary during peak with full lock
