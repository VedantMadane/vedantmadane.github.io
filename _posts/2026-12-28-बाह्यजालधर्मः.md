### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **default deny outbound**: safe base  
२. **every allow has owner and expiry**: hygiene  
३. **prefer private link to SaaS**: less public IP  
४. **meter odd destinations and volume**: exfil  
५. **break-glass egress logged and timed**: incident  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- 0.0.0.0/0 out forever  
- Allowlist without owners  
- No DNS logging  
- Vendor via public internet only by habit
