### What this post is about
**HTTP security headers tell the browser how to treat your origin: scripts frames transport and referrers. They are perimeter policy in header form.**

### Topics covered
1. Why headers  
2. Tight script policy  
3. Durable transport  
4. Frame guard  
5. Tight referrer  
6. Feature lockdown  
7. Isolation when needed  
8. Report then enforce  
9. Not a substitute for authz  
10. Authz vs headers vs keys
