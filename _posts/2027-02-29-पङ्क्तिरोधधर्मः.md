### What this post is about
Backpressure is mercy to the store: when the queue is full the gate closes, clients hear truth and pressure drains upward instead of crushing the root.

### Topics
1. Why fence the queue
2. Close only when full
3. Method: full close empty open
4. Honest signal on block
5. Measured not eternal fence
6. Fence high first
7. Written queue bound
8. Drill the fence
9. Not silent drop
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
पङ्क्तिरोधधर्मः = dharma of queue-line fencing (backpressure).
