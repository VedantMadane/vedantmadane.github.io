### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **append immutable facts only**: append  
२. **projections are disposable views**: proj  
३. **rebuild from stream when view drifts**: rebuild  
४. **upcast old events on read path**: upcast  
५. **snapshots accelerate not replace log**: snap  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Edit past events in place  
- Projection as sole source of truth  
- No rebuild path  
- Delete stream to fix bug silent
