<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **hot keys only**: not full corpus  
२. **TTL invalidate**: freshness  
३. **source-first writes**: no false hits  
४. **coalesce stampedes**: protect origin  
५. **observe hit rate**: no blind cache  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Cache-as-database  
- No TTL on mutable data  
- Stampede on expiry  
- Long secrets in shared RAM
