### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **show raw once**: store only a digest  
२. **narrow scope and env**: no god key  
३. **overlap validity**: no flag-day cut  
४. **limits per key**: isolate noisy clients  
५. **owner and last-used**: expire idle keys  
६. **one-click revoke**: after a leak  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- plaintext key retrieval later  
- long-lived keys in a browser app  
- one universal key for every job
