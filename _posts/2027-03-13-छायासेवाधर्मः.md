### What this post is about
Shadow-service dharma is honest dark observation. Clone live requests into a twin that cannot write production, compare fruit on a beat and kill shadow on fault without touching the real path.

### Topics
1. Why hold a shadow
2. Write shadow not full touch
3. Method: hold watch compare
4. Watch shadow fruit
5. Kill shadow on fault
6. Expand only when proven
7. Write the shadow bound first
8. Name the failure
9. Not full-touch pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
छायासेवाधर्मः = dharma of shadow service.
