### What this post is about
Short-seal dharma is honest short-lived right. Grant access with a timed seal, hold only measured duration, watch seal fruit on a beat and burn seals that outlive their work.

### Topics
1. Why use a short seal
2. Hold seal only measured time
3. Method: hold time watch
4. Watch seal fruit
5. Burn seal when time is past
6. Write the seal bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Drop seal when work is done
9. Not endless-seal pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
लघुमुद्राधर्मः = dharma of the short seal (lease / TTL token).
