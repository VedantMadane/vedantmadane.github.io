### What this post is about
**गलाघ्न** replaces a legacy system by routing one capability at a time through a facade until the old path has nothing left to serve.

### Topics
1. No night-cut of the tree  
2. Facade routing  
3. One capability per bite  
4. Dual-write then cut  
5. Method of split  
6. Shadow compare  
7. Keep a rollback  
8. Starve new work on the old  
9. Finish the decommission  
10. Gateway versus face versus strangler  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **one outer facade**: split new and old  
२. **one user-visible capability per bite**: not a layer pile  
३. **dual-write or shadow until sameness**: then single writer  
४. **compare answers on a shadow path**: page silent drift  
५. **keep a switch back through peak**: no burned bridge  
६. **new features on the new path only**: starve the old  
७. **decommission when the last bite moves**: no half-dead rota  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- a six-month freeze then a weekend cutover  
- forever dual-write with no owner on sameness  
- new product work still landing in the dying service
