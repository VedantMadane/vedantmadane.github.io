<details>
<summary>English · overview and topics (minimizable)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Strangler fig** migrates a legacy system by routing and reimplementing one business capability at a time behind a facade until the old core can die.

### Topics covered
1. Avoid big-bang rewrite  
2. Facade intercept early  
3. Vertical capability seams  
4. Move data with the slice  
5. Flags and canaries  
6. Shadow parity proof  
7. Retire on a calendar  
8. Anti-corruption layer  
9. Per-capability traffic %  
10. Dual write vs strangler vs schema  

### Sanskrit title
**गलाघ्नधर्मः** = *galāghna-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the throat-strangler" (strangler fig).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **facade/proxy first**: route switch ready  
२. **vertical capability seams**: not layer cake  
३. **move data ownership with slice**: no eternal shared DB  
४. **shadow parity before flip**: prove behavior  
५. **kill calendar for legacy code/tables**: when traffic zero  
६. **anti-corruption at boundary**: clean new model  
७. **metric % traffic per capability**: progress truth  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- big-bang rewrite weekend with no facade  
- horizontal half-migrate forever  
- strangler with no decommission plan
