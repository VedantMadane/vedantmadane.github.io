### What this post is about
**Error budgets** quantify how much unreliability an objective allows and govern whether you may ship risk or must harden.

### Topics covered
1. Allowed unreliability
2. Indicator objective remainder
3. Multi-window burn alerts
4. Freeze when empty
5. Journey-based indicators
6. Dependency rollup
7. Availability latency quality
8. Toil as burn
9. Transparent dashboards
10. Canary chaos as deliberate spend

### Sanskrit title
**दोषकोषधर्मः** = *doṣa-koṣa-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the fault-treasury" (error budget).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

१. **define indicator then objective**: then remainder  
२. **alert on burn rates**: fast and slow windows  
३. **freeze risky change**: at budget zero  
४. **journey indicators**: over host vanity
