### What this post is about
**Error budgets** quantify how much unreliability an SLO allows and govern whether you may ship risk or must harden.

### Topics covered
1. Allowed unreliability  
2. SLI SLO budget stack  
3. Multi-window burn alerts  
4. Freeze when empty  
5. Journey-based SLIs  
6. Dependency rollup  
7. Availability latency quality  
8. Toil as burn  
9. Transparent dashboards  
10. Canary chaos as deliberate spend  

### Sanskrit title
**दोषकोषधर्मः** = *doṣa-koṣa-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the fault-treasury" (error budget).

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **define SLI then SLO**: then remainder  
२. **alert on burn rates**: fast and slow windows  
३. **freeze risky change**: at budget zero  
४. **journey SLIs**: over CPU vanity  
५. **model dependency impact**: without double count  
६. **separate latency budgets**: when needed  
७. **show budget to product**: shared tradeoffs  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- page on raw CPU with no SLO  
- keep launching features at budget zero  
- SRE-only secret budget nobody else sees
