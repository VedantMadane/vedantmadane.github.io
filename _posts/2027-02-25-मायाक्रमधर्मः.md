### What this post is about
The Mystery Method (M3 Model) formulated by Erik von Markovik divides the romantic interaction into three rigorous linear phases: Attraction (A1 Open, A2 Female-to-Male Interest / DHV, A3 Male-to-Female Interest / Qualification), Comfort (C1 Conversation, C2 Connection / Kino Escalation, C3 Intimacy / Bedroom Bounce) and Seduction (S1 Foreplay, S2 Last-Minute Resistance / LMR, S3 Sex). Skipping phases causes immediate rejection or friendzone entrapment.

### Topics
1. The M3 Triad: Attraction, Comfort and Seduction (A, C, S)
2. Phase A1: The indirect open and group dynamics
3. Phase A2: Demonstrating higher value (DHV) and disarming with negs
4. Phase A3: Qualification and bait-hook-reel-release
5. False Time Constraints (FTC) and the venue bounce
6. Phase C1: Deep rapport and emotional vulnerability
7. Phase C2 and C3: The physical kino escalation ladder
8. Phase S1: The private sanctuary and isolation
9. Phase S2: Last-Minute Resistance (LMR) and the freeze-out
10. Phase S3: Full surrender and consummation
11. The Cardinal Rule: Calibration and strict phase adherence
12. The final seal of the Mystery Method

### Sanskrit title
मायाक्रमधर्मः = the dharma of the Mystery sequence (the M3 model).

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **त्रिचरणक्रमः**: strict adherence to Attraction, Comfort and Seduction in sequence  
२. **गणप्रवेशः**: opening the group indirectly without early romantic display

४. **स्थानपरिवर्तनम्**: venue bounces to compress perceived relationship time  
५. **स्तम्भनम्**: freezing out upon last-minute resistance rather than forcing  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Attempting seduction before attraction is solidly established  
- Directly hitting on the target while ignoring her social group  
- Supplicating to beauty instead of making her qualify  
- Staying in one venue for hours without moving logistics  
- Arguing or aggressively pushing through last-minute resistance
