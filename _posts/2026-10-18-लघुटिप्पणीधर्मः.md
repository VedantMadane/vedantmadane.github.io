<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **stable fields**: not prose-only  
२. **correlation id required**: on request paths  
३. **stable names**: schema review  
४. **redact secrets**: minimize personal data  
५. **sample successes**: keep errors rich  
६. **retention policy**: often shorter for logs  
७. **alert on fields**: not brittle regex  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- full auth headers in logs  
- unique field name per user  
- info log every packet at full rate with no sample
