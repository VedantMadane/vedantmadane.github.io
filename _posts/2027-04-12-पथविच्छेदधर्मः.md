### What this post is about
Path-cut dharma is honest circuit break. Cut the path when disease rises, watch cut fruit on a beat, stop the sick path and never treat disease-touch as safe dharma.

### Topics
1. Why cut the path
2. Cut on disease do not touch
3. Method: hold cut stop watch
4. Watch cut fruit
5. Stop the sick path
6. Write the cut bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Join path when calm is proven
9. Not disease-touch pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
पथविच्छेदधर्मः = dharma of path-cut (circuit break).
