### What this post is about
Read-throttle dharma is honest backpressure on the read path. Measure read load, watch read fruit on a beat, stop when pace runs past the bound and never treat endless read as safe.

### Topics
1. Why throttle read
2. Measure read do not run endless
3. Method: hold throttle measure watch
4. Watch read fruit
5. Stop excess pace
6. Write the read bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Release read when calm is proven
9. Not endless-read pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
पठनरोधधर्मः = dharma of read-throttle (read backpressure).
