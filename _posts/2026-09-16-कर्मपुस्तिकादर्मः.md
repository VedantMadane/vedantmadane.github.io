### ग्राह्यम्
१. **आह्वानसन्धिः**: every page links a book  
२. **क्रमशःपदानि**: numbered doable steps  
३. **परीक्षा**: game-day execute the book  
४. **जीर्णताचिह्नम्**: last verified date  
५. **स्वचालनसन्धिः**: toil steps become code  
६. **पश्चमार्गः**: rollback/stop conditions  
७. **स्वाम्यम्**: owner for the book  

### त्याज्यम्
- novel-length unread books  
- runbooks never opened in drills  
- alerts with no link
