### What this post is about
A **कर्मपुस्तिका** is a short owned path for a fire. It is not a decision record and not a novel.

### Topics
1. No path means fog
2. Short steps
3. Owner and date
4. Drill in calm
5. Method of a path
6. Write the fruit
7. No secrets
8. Kill stale paths
9. Decision is the why
10. Why versus how
11. Five limbs
12. Close

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **write short runnable steps**: not a poem  
२. **name an owner and a date**: orphan paths rot  
३. **drill in peacetime**: first read must not be the fire  
४. **write the expected fruit**: a wish is not a check  
५. **keep secrets out**: a public book is a door  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- file chat as the runbook  
- first open the book in the fire  
- paste keys into the page
