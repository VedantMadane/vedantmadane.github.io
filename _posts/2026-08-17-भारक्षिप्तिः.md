### ग्राह्यम्
१. **अतिभारचिह्नम्**: queue depth, latency, CPU, error budget burn.  
२. **प्राथमिकी**: critical paths kept; optional work shed first.  
३. **स्वेच्छानिषेधः**: refuse early with clear signal.  
४. **कोष्ठभेदः**: isolate pools so one class cannot eat all.  
५. **न्याय्यक्षयः**: deterministic policy not random cruelty.  
६. **पुनरुत्थानम्**: shed less as load returns to green.  
७. **ग्राहकसन्देशः**: 503/429 with retry guidance when apt.

### त्याज्यम्
- drop random live payments while keeping debug traffic.  
- only adding machines without an admission story.  
- silent blackhole without metrics.
