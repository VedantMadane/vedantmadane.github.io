### What this post is about
Blue-green dharma is honest dual-path release. Hold two live stacks, serve one, test the green first, cut over slowly and reverse without pride.

### Topics
1. Why keep two paths
2. Light only one
3. Method: dual light measure
4. Test green first
5. Cut over in measure
6. Keep blue until green is proven
7. Keep rollback ready
8. Name the failure
9. Not single-path pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
नीलीहरितधर्मः = dharma of blue-green dual path.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **keep two live deploy paths**: dual  
२. **serve traffic on one only**: one light  
३. **test green before the cut**: test  
४. **cut over in measured steps**: measure  
५. **keep rollback wired and ready**: rollback  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Cut over with one path only  
- Serve both stacks at once without rule  
- Skip green pre-check  
- Drop blue before green is proven  
- No ready reverse path
