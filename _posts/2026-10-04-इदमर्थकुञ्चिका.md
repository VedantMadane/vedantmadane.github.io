<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **client mints before first try**: reuse on every retry  
२. **scope tenant + op + intent**: never share across payments  
३. **store the success record**: replay do not redo  
४. **document TTL expiry**: new intent or reject  
५. **lock the first writer**: loser reads the winner  
६. **skip keys on pure reads**: focus side-effecting writes  
७. **forward a stable upstream key**: do not double-charge providers  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- server mints a new key per try  
- require keys on GET  
- silent redo after stored success
