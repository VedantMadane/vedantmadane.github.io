### What this post is about
**Read replicas** absorb SELECT load from a primary. Correct use needs lag awareness and split routing.

### Topics covered
1. Primary overload from reads  
2. Replication lag  
3. Writes only on primary  
4. read-your-writes vs stale-ok  
5. Lag metrics and failover of reads  
6. Schema migrations under lag  
7. Replica vs backup  
8. Separate pools  
9. Multi-region lag  
10. Capacity: reads vs writes  

### Sanskrit title
**वाचनप्रतिलिपिः** = *vācana-pratilipiḥ* "reading-copy / read replica".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **लेखप्राथमिकः**: all writes to writer  
२. **पठननीतिः**: explicit stale vs strong  
३. **lagSLO**: max lag for replica use  
४. **पृथक्कुण्डम्**: separate read pool  
५. **प्रवाससङ्गतिः**: expand/contract under lag  
६. **मापनम्**: lag bytes/seconds  
७. **HA विवेकः**: replica promotion is another runbook  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- always read replica after write without sticky  
- treat replica as backup only  
- one pool to mixed endpoints
