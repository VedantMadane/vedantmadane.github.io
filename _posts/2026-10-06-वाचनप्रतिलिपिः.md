<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **all writes to primary**: never write to a replica  
२. **name stale-ok paths**: read-your-writes on primary  
३. **monitor lag against SLO**: fail closed to primary  
४. **keep migrations compatible**: across primary and replicas  
५. **split pools by role**: analytics must not block checkout  
६. **do not confuse replica and backup**: point-in-time is another job  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- treat a replica as instantly same  
- write to replicas  
- use one pool for primary and replica
