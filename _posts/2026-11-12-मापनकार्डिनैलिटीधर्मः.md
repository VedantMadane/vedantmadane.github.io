<details>

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Metrics cardinality** is the count of unique time series created by label combinations. Unbounded labels are an availability and cost incident in slow motion.

### Topics covered
1. Series explosion risk
2. Labels as finite dimensions
3. Per-service series budgets
4. Aggregate plus exemplars
5. Path template normalization
6. Growth detection alerts
7. Drop at scrape edge
8. Bounded histogram buckets
9. Metrics vs events for detail
10. Trace vs metric vs budget roles

### Sanskrit title
**मापनकार्डिनैलिटीधर्मः** = *māpana-kārḍinailiṭī-dharmaḥ* "dharma of measurement cardinality". Verses use **मापनबाहुल्यम्**.

### English

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **finite enum labels only**: no user ids  
२. **series budget per service**: enforce  
३. **counters/histograms first**: not gauge-per-entity  
४. **template paths/methods**: before label  
५. **alert on series growth**: before invoice  
६. **relabel/drop at edge**: not after store  
७. **bounded hist buckets**: intentional  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- label raw URLs and user ids  
- unlimited custom metrics with no quota  
- dynamic unbounded histogram buckets as default
