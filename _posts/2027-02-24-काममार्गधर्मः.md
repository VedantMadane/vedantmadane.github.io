### What this post is about
Attraction cannot be begged for or negotiated through logical supplication. This treatise codifies the universal architecture of Game: Access through cold approach, Attraction through unapologetic polarity, Emotional tension through the F.R.E.D. model (Focus, Rhythm, Emotion and Drama), Calibration through micro-expression discernment and decisive Follow-Through into intimacy.

### Topics
1. The core paradox of game and outcome independence
2. Access: stepping into unfamiliar avenues
3. Cold approach: halting the moving maiden with frontal poise
4. Physical and vocal grounding: slow throat resonance
5. Polarity: masculine essence over logical debate
6. F.R.E.D. Focus and Rhythm: locking gaze and slow tempo
7. F.R.E.D. Emotion and Drama: feeling state and teasing
8. Sexual tension vs anxious comfort: holding the silence
9. Qualification and frame: judging value without supplication
10. Physical escalation and calibration: incremental touch
11. Follow-through and logistics: leading from street to privacy
12. The final seal: victory in the art of desire

### Sanskrit title
काममार्गधर्मः = the dharma of the path of desire and courtship.
