<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Canary releases** send a sticky handful of real users to a new version and widen only while health and product signals stay good against the concurrent baseline. This is not shadow (no users) and not blue-green (whole fleet).

### Topics
1. Bounded blast radius  
2. Sticky cohorts  
3. Auto promote and rollback  
4. Slice versus live baseline  
5. Bake time per step  
6. Shared store and schema risk  
7. Staff force path  
8. Business signals money cares about  
9. Risk-tuned ladders  
10. Shadow then slice then fleet  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya in verses.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **sticky cohorts**: one workflow one version  
२. **compare to live baseline**: not last week alone  
३. **bake each step**: enough wall time for rares  
४. **auto rollback**: on budget burn  
५. **expand-contract schemas**: old and new coexist  
६. **watch checkout signals**: green machines can hide a dead funnel  
७. **publish ladders**: slower for money and auth  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- jump from none to half with no bake  
- ship a new app that breaks a shared schema  
- ignore funnel metrics when machines look green
