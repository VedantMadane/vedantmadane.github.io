### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **uniform mTLS and timeouts**: fleet-wide  
२. **retries with budget**: no amplification  
३. **telemetry without app rewrites**: golden signals  
४. **pay sidecar CPU honestly**: measure cost  
५. **prefer gateway if only north-south**: simpler path  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Mesh for two services only  
- Infinite retries  
- mTLS off in prod  
- Ignoring sidecar cost
