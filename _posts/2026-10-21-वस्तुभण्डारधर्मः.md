### What this post is about
**Object storage** keeps immutable-ish blobs under keys with HTTP APIs lifecycle rules and durability features.

### Topics covered
1. Why not only disks  
2. Key layout and hotspots  
3. Lifecycle tiers  
4. Versioning / guarded delete  
5. Presigned access  
6. Multipart cleanup  
7. Consistency models  
8. Encryption custody  
9. Inventory and cost metrics  
10. Links to upload CDC lakes  

### Sanskrit title
**वस्तुभण्डारधर्मः** = *vastu-bhaṇḍāra-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the object store".

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **prefix design**: avoid hot keys  
२. **lifecycle rules**: hot/cool/expire  
३. **versioning**: recovery aid  
४. **short presign**: least privilege  
५. **abort multiparts**: cost control  
६. **encrypt + key policy**: custody clear  
७. **metrics**: requests errors growth  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- public-read on sensitive prefixes  
- no lifecycle ever  
- leave incomplete multiparts forever
