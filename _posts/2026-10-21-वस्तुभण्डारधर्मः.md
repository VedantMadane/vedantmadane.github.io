### What this post is about
**Object stores hold opaque blobs addressable by key with durability and HTTP APIs. They fit large binary and lake workloads better than block disks alone.**

### Topics covered
1. Why the house of objects  
2. Key layout and heat  
3. Lifecycle tiers  
4. Versioned delete  
5. Timed access  
6. Multipart cleanup  
7. Consistency  
8. Encryption custody  
9. Metrics  
10. Door vs house

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **design keys for list and heat**: avoid sequential hotspots  
२. **lifecycle tiers**: expire or cool  
३. **version deletes**: recover from wipe  
४. **short timed access**: no public secret prefix  
५. **abort leftover parts**: or the bill grows  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- public bucket for private prefixes  
- endless incomplete multiparts  
- treat list as always linearizable
