### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **encrypt rest and transit by default**: both  
२. **envelope DEK/KEK hierarchy**: blast radius  
३. **rotate without rewriting universe carefully**: ops  
४. **separate key ACL from data ACL**: least  
५. **log key use and failures**: forensics  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Crypto checkbox only at rest  
- Keys beside ciphertext in same bucket  
- No rotate path  
- Custom ciphers for fun
