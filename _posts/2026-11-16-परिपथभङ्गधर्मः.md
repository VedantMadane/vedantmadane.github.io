### What this post is about
**Circuit breakers** temporarily stop calling a failing dependency and fail fast or fall back until a controlled probe succeeds.

### Topics covered
1. Stop repeated timeout tax  
2. Closed open half-open  
3. Rate-based trip conditions  
4. Fail-fast fallback when open  
5. Limited half-open probes  
6. Per-dependency scope  
7. Compose with bulkhead and timeout  
8. State and flap metrics  
9. Manual force-open  
10. Bulkhead vs breaker vs retry  

### Sanskrit title
**परिपथभङ्गधर्मः** = *paripatha-bhaṅga-dharmaḥ* "dharma of breaking the circuit path".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **three states with half-open**: probe recovery  
२. **trip on sustained rates**: not one blip  
३. **fail fast with fallback**: while open  
४. **limit half-open trials**: no stampede  
५. **breaker per dependency**: not one global  
६. **pair timeout+bulkhead**: layered  
७. **manual force-open**: for incidents  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- retry storms with no breaker on dead dep  
- forever-open without half-open probe  
- one breaker for all outbound calls
