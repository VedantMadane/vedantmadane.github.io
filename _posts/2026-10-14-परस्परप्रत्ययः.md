<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **both sides present certs**: identity is not a private IP  
२. **automate issuance and rotation**: manual multi-year certs become outages  
३. **pick mesh or app termination**: doing both without need wastes CPU  
४. **monitor expiry fleet-wide**: one expiry can fail all handshakes  
५. **map identity to allow-lists**: proof of who is not proof of what  
६. **accept previous and next during rollout**: stale trust stores break a fraction  
७. **fence any plain debug path**: do not leave east-west open  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- trust the LAN because it is private  
- manual yearly client certs with no monitor  
- disable mutual cover in production for convenience
