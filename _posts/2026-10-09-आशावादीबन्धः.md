<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **updates include the version read**: zero rows means someone else won  
२. **speak a version mark on the door**: conditional write  
३. **return a clear conflict**: never silent last-write-wins on money  
४. **help the client reload and merge**: do not loop write until it sticks  
५. **pessimistic locks for short hot sections**: optimistic for rare writes  
६. **per-item versions in batches**: state abort-all or partial  
७. **high conflict means too hot**: shard queue or lock  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- silent last-write-wins on money  
- omit version on update  
- treat keys as a substitute for versions
