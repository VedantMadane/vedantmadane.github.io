### What this post is about
Every durable write can speak again as a stream. Capture at the source, advance offsets with care, never fork truth silently.

### Topics
1. Why CDC
2. Source capture
3. Offset commit
4. At-least-once sink
5. Schema on stream
6. Backfill
7. Delete as event
8. Lag page
9. Not dual source of truth
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **capture at primary write path**: source  
२. **commit offsets after sink ack**: offset  
३. **sinks tolerate duplicates**: alo  
४. **deletes are first-class events**: delete  
५. **page on capture lag**: lag  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Poll tables as only CDC forever  
- Commit offset before sink  
- Drop deletes silently  
- Two independent write truths
