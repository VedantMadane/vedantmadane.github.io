### What this post is about
A **बहिर्लेखनपटलः** is how a message is born with the business write. Two separate writes invent a second truth.

### Topics
1. Dual write invents a lie
2. Same bind as the act
3. Relay not womb-carry
4. At-least-once with a mark
5. Method of a board
6. Mark the sent
7. Keep order
8. Not womb-stream birth
9. Inbox is take outbox is birth
10. Three jobs
11. Five limbs
12. Close

Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **write act and message in one bind**: not two later writes  
२. **relay out of the womb**: do not carry the net in the request  
३. **mark sent**: a markless line doubles  
४. **keep order**: a shuffled line kills truth  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- dual write to store and bus  
- publish inside the request womb  
- no sent-mark then retry forever
