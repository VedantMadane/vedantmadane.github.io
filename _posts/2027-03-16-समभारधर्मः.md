### What this post is about
Even-load dharma is honest traffic spread. Hold many vessels, distribute in measure, watch each vessel on a beat and never crown a sick vessel as healthy.

### Topics
1. Why spread the load
2. Distribute never one vessel
3. Method: hold distribute watch
4. Watch vessel fruit
5. Skip the sick vessel
6. Write the load bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Lead load only on proven paths
9. Not single-vessel pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
समभारधर्मः = dharma of even load (balanced distribution).
