<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **prefer hard prod separation**: if multi-env then strict policy and no shared secrets  
२. **attach quotas and default limits**: one team cannot schedule away the cluster  
३. **least privilege inside the room**: no casual cluster-admin  
४. **include network policy**: not only directory labels  
५. **never mount prod secrets into dev**: automate sealed distribution per env  
६. **standard team and env labels**: policy and cost allocation work  
७. **high-risk tenants may need cells**: namespaces are not perfect hostile isolation  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one shared room for all envs  
- cluster-admin for app teams  
- labels without network policy
