### What this post is about
**Namespaces** compartmentalize teams and environments inside an orchestrator cluster using policy quotas and network rules.

### Topics covered
1. Noisy neighbor in one bag  
2. dev/stage/prod separation  
3. ResourceQuota and LimitRange  
4. Namespaced RBAC  
5. Default-deny NetworkPolicy  
6. Secret boundaries  
7. Label standards  
8. Soft namespace vs hard isolation  
9. Per-namespace usage metrics  
10. Stack with pod quotas and multi-tenant dharma  

### Sanskrit title
**नामस्थानधर्मः** = *nāmasthāna-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the name-place / namespace".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **env namespaces**: prod hard split preferred  
२. **ResourceQuota**: per ns budgets  
३. **Least RBAC**: no casual cluster-admin  
४. **default-deny net**: explicit allows  
५. **secret hygiene**: no cross-ns prod secrets  
६. **label standard**: cost and policy  
७. **usage dashboards**: per ns  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- shared prod/dev namespace  
- flat allow-all CNI as multi-tenant safety  
- unlimited objects per ns
