### What this post is about
Dead-letter dharma is honest quarantine of failed work. Hold a death sheet for failed writes, keep them off the root stream, inspect on a beat and retry only with a written bound.

### Topics
1. Why hold a death sheet
2. Lead failed write apart
3. Method: hold apart watch
4. Watch letter fruit
5. Retry the letter in measure
6. Write the letter bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Never touch root from a proven letter fault
9. Not no-sheet pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
मृत्युपत्रधर्मः = dharma of the dead-letter sheet.
