### What this post is about
Fruit-compare dharma is honest reconciliation of outcomes. Compare pair fruit on a beat, name every split, reject false fruit as truth and never treat uncompared state as settled.

### Topics
1. Why compare fruit
2. Compare on a beat
3. Method: hold compare name
4. Name the split when seen
5. Reject false fruit as truth
6. Write the compare bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Guard fruit when same is proven
9. Not no-compare pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
फलतुलनाधर्मः = dharma of fruit compare (reconciliation).
