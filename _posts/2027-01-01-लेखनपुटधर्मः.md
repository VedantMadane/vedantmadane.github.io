When a service must change state and announce it, the outbox keeps one truth until the message really leaves.

1. Why outbox
2. Same transaction

5. Ordering keys
6. Poison rows
7. Metrics on lag
8. Not dual HTTP
9. Vs event store
10. Five limbs
11. Close

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **state+outbox row one commit**: atomic  
२. **relay with backoff and cursor**: safe  
३. **consumers idempotent**: replay  
४. **never dual-write bus and DB**: ban  
५. **page on outbox lag**: ops  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- DB commit then best-effort HTTP  
- No relay metrics  
- Outbox table forever unrelayed  
- Dual write both sides
