### What this post is about
State and announce must share one commit. The outbox is the waiting room until the message truly leaves.

### Topics
1. One commit
2. Outbox row
3. Relay cursor
4. Idempotent sink
5. Order keys
6. Poison letter
7. Lag pages
8. Ban dual write
9. Not full event ocean
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title

Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

१. **business write + outbox one TX**: atomic  
२. **relay with durable cursor**: safe  
३. **consumers idempotent**: replay  
४. **never DB then best-effort bus**: ban  
५. **page on unsent age**: ops  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Commit then hope HTTP  
- No lag metric  
- Stuck poison blocks all  
- Dual write both sinks
