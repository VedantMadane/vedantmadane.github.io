Browsers enforce same-origin policy. Cross-origin policy is the explicit server opt-in that allows chosen web apps to read responses.

<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **reflect only allow-listed origins**: never star with credentials  
२. **answer preflights with intended methods**: over-broad invites abuse  
३. **named origin if credentials true**: pair with CSRF on cookie sessions  
४. **send Vary Origin when ACAO varies**: caches must not reuse one origin for another  
५. **centralize at the gateway**: services must not invent conflicting rules  
६. **apply CORS headers on errors too**: or the face hides the real body  
७. **still enforce real authorization**: native clients ignore CORS  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- reflect any Origin  
- star plus credentials  
- treat CORS as the only security control
