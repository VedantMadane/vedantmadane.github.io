### What this post is about
**सेवास्तम्भनम्** is how a process stops without dropping in-flight work or lying about health.

### Topics
1. Sudden kill loses mid-work
2. Stop new accepts first
3. Bound the drain clock
4. Cancel downstream
5. Ordered stop method
6. Fail ready keep live
7. Coded close not silence
8. Crash is last
9. Shed load is not drain
10. Health versus stop versus kill
11. Five limbs
12. Close

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **refuse new work first**: then finish in-flight  
२. **bound the drain clock**: then hard stop  
३. **propagate cancel down**: do not leave orphans  
४. **fail ready keep live**: so balancers leave  
५. **coded close for peers**: reconnect with a sign  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- hard-kill as the daily deploy path  
- wait forever for one stuck call  
- stay Ready while draining
