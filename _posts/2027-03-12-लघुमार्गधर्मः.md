### What this post is about
Short-path canary dharma is honest progressive risk. Route a tiny share, observe short fruit, expand only when proven and never let a short-path fault touch the full path.

### Topics
1. Why use a short path
2. Small share first
3. Method: small watch expand
4. Watch short fruit
5. Kill short path on fault
6. Expand only when proven
7. Write the share bound first
8. Name the failure
9. Not full-first pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
लघुमार्गधर्मः = dharma of the short canary path.
