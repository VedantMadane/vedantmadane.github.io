### What this post is about
**Singleflight** makes concurrent callers for the same expensive work share one in-flight execution. It is the cold-stampede twin of a warm cache.

### Topics
1. Coalesce identical work  
2. Key identity design  
3. Thundering herd and soft life  
4. Shared error policy  
5. Per-waiter timeouts  
6. Reads not unique writes  
7. Suppression metrics  
8. Multi-instance leases  
9. Cancel versus remaining waiters  
10. Cache and backpressure links  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **key by logical work**: not too coarse not too fine  
२. **pair with soft lifetime**: on cache fill  
३. **define error stickiness**: for waiters  
४. **independent waiter deadlines**: a hung leader must not pin all  
५. **reads and pure compute only**: no distinct money writes  
६. **measure suppressions**: prove the pattern pays  
७. **plan multi-instance herd**: short lease or edge coalesce  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- singleflight distinct checkout posts  
- no per-waiter timeout on a hung leader  
- process-local coalesce across a large fleet with no lease
