### What this post is about
**Singleflight** ensures concurrent callers for the same expensive work share one in-flight execution.

### Topics
1. Coalesce identical work
2. Key identity design
3. Thundering herd and soft life
4. Shared error policy
5. Per-waiter timeouts
6. Reads not unique writes
7. Suppression metrics
8. Multi-instance leases
9. Cancel versus remaining waiters
10. Cache and backpressure links
11. Five limbs
12. Close

Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

१. **key by logical work**: not too coarse  
२. **pair with soft lifetime**: on cache fill

४. **independent waiter deadlines**: always  
५. **reads and pure compute only**: no distinct writes  
६. **measure suppressions**: prove value  
७. **plan multi-instance herd**: lease or edge  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid

- no per-waiter timeout on a hung leader  
- local-only coalesce across a large fleet with no lease
