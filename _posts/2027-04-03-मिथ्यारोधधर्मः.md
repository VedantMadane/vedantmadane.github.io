### What this post is about
Falsehood-block dharma is honest integrity at the edge. Stop inputs that wear a false face, watch truth fruit on a beat, never crown a lie as settled and keep the true road when proven.

### Topics
1. Why block falsehood
2. Stop false do not call it truth
3. Method: hold block watch
4. Watch truth fruit
5. Kill false path before full touch
6. Write the block bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Guard road when truth is proven
9. Not false-road pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
मिथ्यारोधधर्मः = dharma of falsehood-block (anti-spoof integrity).
