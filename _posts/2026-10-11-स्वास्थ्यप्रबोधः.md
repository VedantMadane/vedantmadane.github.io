### What this post is about
**Health probes** (liveness readiness startup) tell orchestrators and load balancers whether an instance is alive and ready for traffic.

### Topics covered
1. Dead instances still receiving traffic  
2. Liveness vs readiness  
3. Startup probes  
4. Shallow vs deep dependency checks  
5. Readiness-first on graceful shutdown  
6. Period timeout thresholds  
7. Cheap /health handlers  
8. Probe metrics  
9. Probes vs external synthetics  
10. Deploy gates  

### Sanskrit title
**स्वास्थ्यप्रबोधः** = *svāsthya-prabodhaḥ* "health awakening / health probe".

### English
Minimizable details; Sanskrit-first default.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **त्रिप्रबोधः**: live/ready/start split  
२. **लघुपथः**: cheap health handlers  
३. **मितनिर्भरता**: no cascade-ready-all  
४. **shutdown क्रमः**: ready false then drain  
५. **threshold विवेकः**: avoid flap on blips  
६. **probe metrics**: restarts NotReady  
७. **auth on /health**: if exposed  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one probe does all three jobs  
- health query full table scan  
- liveness fail while still draining
