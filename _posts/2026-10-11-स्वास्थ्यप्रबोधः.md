<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **liveness asks restart**: readiness asks traffic now  
२. **startup probe shields slow boot**: from liveness kills  
३. **optional shallow dep checks**: deep shared checks go NotReady together  
४. **fail readiness first on shutdown**: drain while liveness stays true  
५. **tune period timeout thresholds**: one GC blip must not flap  
६. **cheap locked health handlers**: expensive queries amplify outages  
७. **alert on restarts and NotReady**: synthetics watch the outside journey  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one probe for live and ready  
- deep shared-store check on every tick  
- skip probe metrics on deploys
