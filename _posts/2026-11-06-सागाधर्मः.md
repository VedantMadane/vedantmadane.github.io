### What this post is about
**Sagas** implement long-running business flows across services with local transactions and compensating actions instead of a global lock.

### Topics
1. Why not one global bind
2. Local commit plus outcome
3. Compensation as business reverse
4. Orchestration versus choreography
5. Idempotent steps
6. Stuck-flow visibility
7. Reservations and semantic holds
8. Failure compensate-or-stop
9. When a local bind is enough
10. Dual write versus saga versus outbox
11. Five limbs
12. Close

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

१. **local bind per step**: no global lock fantasy  
२. **business compensations**: not only row undo  
३. **choose thread or dance**: document the map  
४. **idempotent step marks**: duplicate safe  
५. **age open flows**: ops dashboards  
६. **reserve scarce goods**: timed holds  
७. **prefer a single-store bind**: when the invariant fits  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid

- compensation that is not actually reversible  
- no dashboard for open-flow age
