<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
**Sagas** implement long-running business flows across services with local transactions and compensating actions instead of a global lock.

### Topics covered
1. Why not one global TX  
2. Local commit plus outcome  
3. Compensation as business reverse  
4. Orchestration vs choreography  
5. Idempotent steps  
6. Stuck saga visibility  
7. Reservations and semantic locks  
8. Failure compensate-or-stop  
9. When a local TX is enough  
10. Dual write vs saga vs outbox  

### Sanskrit title
**सागाधर्मः** = *sāgā-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the saga".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **local TX per step**: no global XA fantasy  
२. **business compensations**: not only row undo  
३. **choose orch or choreography**: document map  
४. **idempotent step ids**: duplicate safe  
५. **age open sagas**: ops dashboards  
६. **reserve scarce goods**: TTL holds  
७. **prefer single-DB TX**: when invariant fits  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- hold distributed locks across SaaS APIs  
- compensation that is not actually reversible  
- no dashboard for open saga age
