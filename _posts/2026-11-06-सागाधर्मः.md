### Topics
1. Why not one global bind  
2. Local commit plus outcome  
3. Compensation as business reverse  
4. Orchestration versus choreography  
5. Idempotent steps  
6. Stuck-flow visibility  
7. Reservations and semantic holds  
8. Failure compensate-or-stop  
9. When a local bind is enough  
10. Dual write versus saga versus outbox  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya. Title keeps सागा; verses prefer क्रमगाथा.

१. **local bind per step**: no global lock fantasy

३. **choose thread or dance**: document the map  
४. **idempotent step marks**: duplicate safe  
५. **age open flows**: ops dashboards

**English sense**  
A long flow across services cannot hold one global bind. Locks time out, partners do not speak your transaction protocol and the user hangs. Sequence local commits and compensate later steps. That is the saga. Two-phase commit across alien systems is a wish.

Topic: why a saga. Distinct from a local transaction in one store.
