2. Bound name-growth
3. Short retain and downsample
4. Split write and read
5. Method of a point
6. Labels stay small
7. Roll up the old
8. Not a tale warehouse
9. A log is a tale a point is a clock
10. Three jobs
11. Five limbs
12. Close
