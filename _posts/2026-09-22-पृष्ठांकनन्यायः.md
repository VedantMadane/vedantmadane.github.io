4. No heavy total

6. Tell a true end
7. Bound the household

<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **prefer a cursor**: offset skips and doubles on a moving list  
२. **keep order stable**: unstable sort is a skip  
३. **do not count the world on the request**: a heavy total stalls the journey  
४. **tell a true end**: a fake end loops the client  
५. **bound per household**: one customer cannot walk the whole store  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- offset into a live changing list  
- return a fake next mark forever  
- compute exact totals on every page
