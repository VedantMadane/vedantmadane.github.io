### What this post is about
**A remote cache stores hot data outside the app process so many instances share one hot set and spare the database.**

### Topics covered
1. Why shared memory  
2. One pattern per resource  
3. Jittered expiry  
4. Versioned payloads  
5. Memory ceiling  
6. Hot keys  
7. Not the system of record  
8. Tenant prefixes  
9. Hit and eviction metrics  
10. Cache vs edge  

### Sanskrit title

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **one pattern per resource**: do not mix aside and write-through  
२. **jittered TTL**: singleflight on miss  
३. **memory ceiling**: eviction policy  
४. **tenant prefix mandatory**: privacy  
५. **rebuild from the database**: after total loss  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- cache as only copy of business truth  
- missing tenant prefix  
- unlimited growth as if it were a store
