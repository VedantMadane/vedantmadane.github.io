### What this post is about
**Chaos engineering** deliberately injects faults under control to validate that steady-state user outcomes hold.

### Topics covered
1. Learn before night pages
2. Hypothesis before inject
3. Staging then guarded live
4. Varied fault catalog
5. Small blast radius
6. Customer-journey success
7. Continuous not annual-only
8. Owned remediation
9. Freeze and peak safety
10. Shed vs chaos vs canary

### Sanskrit title
**यन्त्रनाशधर्मः** = *yantra-nāśa-dharmaḥ* "dharma of deliberate machine-breaking".

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **hypothesis and abort rules**: before inject  
२. **earn live with staging**: and a stop button  
३. **vary latency dep disk**: not only kill  
४. **small blast first**: grow later  
५. **judge user journeys**: not only pods  
६. **track fixes to owners**: no shelf reports  
७. **honor freezes and peaks**: no sale chaos  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- random live kills with no hypothesis  
- region-wide first experiment  
- chaos during peak checkout with no abort rule
