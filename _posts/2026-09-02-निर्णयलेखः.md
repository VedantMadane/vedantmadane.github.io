### ग्राह्यम्
१. **लघुलेखः**: one decision one record  
२. **विकल्पस्मृतिः**: options considered named  
३. **परिणामाः**: tradeoffs explicit  
४. **स्थितिः**: proposed accepted superseded  
५. **सन्दर्भः**: links to code/PRs  
६. **जीर्णता**: supersede don't rewrite history silently  
७. **खोज्यता**: findable near the system  

### त्याज्यम्
- decisions only in meeting memory  
- rewrite ADR to hide regret  
- 100-page ADRs nobody reads
