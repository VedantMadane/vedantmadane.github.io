<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### What this post is about
A **निर्णयलेखः** is a short owned record of a choice that later people can replay without the original room.

### Topics
1. Unwritten choices return as fog
2. Options and fruits
3. Status of the choice
4. Owner and date
5. Method of a short record
6. Readable not a treatise
7. Record reversals
8. Secrets stay out
9. Meeting notes are not the record
10. Record versus order versus chat
11. Five limbs
12. Close

### English
Minimizable; Sanskrit-first; pure padya.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **write the why**: not only the what  
२. **name refused options**: and their fruits  
३. **give a status**: accepted superseded or rejected  
४. **name an owner and a date**: orphan notes rot  
५. **record reversals with a why**: silent flips breed fog  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- treat chat as the archive  
- write a novel nobody reads  
- put secrets in the public record
