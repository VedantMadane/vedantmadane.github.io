## प्रथमाध्यायः - मूल्याङ्कन-दर्शनम् (Chapter 1: Philosophy & Process)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 1 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v1">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">मूल्यं विज्ञाय सर्वस्य क्रीणीयात् तन्न चाधिकम्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">सम्पदामार्जनं कुर्यात् धनप्रवाहहेतुना॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> Having ascertained the intrinsic value of any asset, an investor should not pay more than it is worth. One should acquire assets fundamentally for the cashflows they are expected to generate.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">मूल्यं (value) विज्ञाय (having known) सर्वस्य (of all) क्रीणीयात् (one should buy) तत् (that) न च (and not) अधिकम् (more). सम्पदाम् (of assets) आर्जनं (acquisition) कुर्यात् (one should do) धन-प्रवाह-हेतुना (for the sake of cashflows).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 2 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v2">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">पूर्वग्रहोऽनिश्चयश्च काठिन्यं दोषहेतवः।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">न्यूनेन यत् सिद्ध्यति तत् बहुभिर्नैव साधयेत्॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> Analyst bias, inherent uncertainty and model complexity are the primary sources of valuation error. According to the principle of parsimony, that which can be accomplished with fewer inputs should not be overcomplicated with many.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">पूर्वग्रहः (bias) अनिश्चयः (uncertainty) च (and) काठिन्यं (complexity) दोष-हेतवः (causes of error). न्यूनेन (by less/simple) यत् (what) सिद्ध्यति (is accomplished) तत् (that) बहुभिः (by many) न एव (never) साधयेत् (should one attempt).</div>
</details>

## द्वितीयाध्यायः - मूल्याङ्कन-मार्गाः (Chapter 2: Approaches to Valuation)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 3 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v3">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">मूल्याङ्कनस्य त्रैविध्यं मार्गेषु दृश्यते बुधैः।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">अपकर्षितमुद्राभिः मूल्यमानं तु पूर्वकम्॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The wise observe three broad approaches to valuation. The first approach measures value via discounted cashflows.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">मूल्याङ्कनस्य (of valuation) त्रैविध्यं (threefold nature) मार्गेषु (among the approaches) दृश्यते (is seen) बुधैः (by the wise). अपकर्षित-मुद्राभिः (by discounted cashflows) मूल्य-मानं (measuring of value) तु (indeed) पूर्वकम् (is the first).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 4 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v4">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">सापेक्षं मूल्यमानं च तुल्यसम्पत्प्रमाणतः।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">सापाधिकं विकल्पेन कृष्णशोलनयेन च॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The second is relative valuation, standardizing the pricing based on comparable assets. The third is contingent claim valuation, using option pricing methods like the Black-Scholes model.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">सापेक्षं (relative) मूल्य-मानं (valuation) च (and) तुल्य-सम्पत्-प्रमाणतः (from the standard of comparable assets). सापाधिकं (contingent claim) विकल्पेन (by options) कृष्ण-शोल-नयेन (by the Black-Scholes method) च (and).</div>
</details>

## तृतीयाध्यायः - मूल्याङ्कन-प्रयोजनानि (Chapter 3: The Role of Valuation)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 5 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v5">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">सञ्चयस्य प्रबन्धे च तथा सङ्ग्रहणेऽपि च।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">निगमस्य वित्तशास्त्रे मूल्यज्ञानं प्रयोज्यते॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The knowledge of valuation is applied in active portfolio management, in the analysis of corporate acquisitions and across the spectrum of corporate finance.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">सञ्चयस्य (of the portfolio) प्रबन्धे (in the management) च (and) तथा (as well as) सङ्ग्रहणे (in acquisitions) अपि च (and also). निगमस्य (of the corporation) वित्त-शास्त्रे (in the financial science/corporate finance) मूल्य-ज्ञानं (the knowledge of value) प्रयोज्यते (is applied).</div>
</details>

## चतुर्थाध्यायः - जोखिम-पूँजीमूल्य-विचारः (Chapter 4: Risk and the Cost of Capital)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 6 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v6">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">विपत्तिश्च सुयोगश्च जोखिमं परिकीर्तितम्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">प्रत्याशितफलाद् भेदो मूल्याङ्कने विचिन्त्यते॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> In valuation, risk is defined as the variance of actual returns from expected returns, encompassing both danger and opportunity.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">विपत्तिः (danger) च (and) सुयोगः (opportunity) च (and) जोखिमं (risk) परिकीर्तितम् (is declared). प्रत्याशित-फलात् (from the expected outcome) भेदः (difference/variance) मूल्याङ्कने (in valuation) विचिन्त्यते (is considered).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 7 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v7">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">निर्बाधलाभमानेन विपणिप्रतिदानतः।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">बीटागुणेन संयुक्तं स्वपूँजीमूल्यमुच्यते॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The cost of equity is estimated by combining a risk-free rate with a market risk premium that is multiplied by the asset's beta. The marginal investor is assumed to be fully diversified, meaning only non-diversifiable market risk is rewarded.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">निर्बाध-लाभ-मानेन (With the measure of the risk-free return) विपणि-प्रतिदानतः (from the market premium). बीटा-गुणेन (by the multiplier of beta) संयुक्तं (combined) स्वपूँजी-मूल्यम् (the cost of equity) उच्यते (is said).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 8 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v8">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">व्यापारस्य स्वभावश्च स्थिरव्ययप्रमाणकम्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">ऋणभारस्तथा चैव बीटामानस्य हेतवः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> A firm's fundamental beta is determined by the cyclical nature of its business, its degree of operating leverage (fixed costs) and its financial leverage.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">व्यापारस्य (of the business) स्वभावः (nature/type) च (and) स्थिर-व्यय-प्रमाणकम् (the proportion of fixed costs/operating leverage). ऋण-भारः (the burden of debt/financial leverage) तथा च एव (and also) बीटा-मानस्य (of the beta value) हेतवः (are the causes).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 9 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v9">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">ऋणमूल्यं तु निर्णीतं व्यतिक्रमभयाश्रितम्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">करलाभेन संक्षीणं तदन्ते परिकल्प्यते॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The cost of debt is based on the firm's default risk and the corresponding default spread. This cost is then reduced by the marginal tax rate to reflect the tax-deductibility of interest, yielding the after-tax cost of debt.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">ऋण-मूल्यं (the cost of debt) तु (indeed) निर्णीतं (is determined) व्यतिक्रम-भयाश्रितम् (based on the fear of default/default risk). कर-लाभेन (by the tax benefit) संक्षीणं (diminished/reduced) तदन्ते (at the end/after-tax) परिकल्प्यते (it is calculated).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 10 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v10">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">पूँजीमूल्यं समग्रं हि भाराक्रान्तं विधीयते।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">पुस्तकीयप्रमाणैर्न विपणिमूल्यमानतः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The overall cost of capital is computed as a weighted average of the costs of different financing components. These weights must strictly be based on current market values, not historical book values.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">पूँजी-मूल्यं (the cost of capital) समग्रं (overall) हि (indeed) भाराक्रान्तं (weighted) विधीयते (is made). पुस्तकीय-प्रमाणैः (by book value proportions) न (not) विपणि-मूल्य-मानतः (by the measure of market value).</div>
</details>

## पञ्चमाध्यायः - धनप्रवाह-मापनम् (Chapter 5: Measuring Cash Flows)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 11 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v11">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">आयं संशोध्य यत्नेन करान् दत्त्वा च वर्धयेत्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">अवशिष्टं धनं यत् स्यात् प्रवाहः स प्रकीर्तितः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> Having diligently corrected accounting income, paid corporate taxes and accounted for reinvestment needs, the residual wealth left over is declared as the true free cash flow.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">आयं (income/earnings) संशोध्य (having corrected) यत्नेन (with effort) करान् (taxes) दत्त्वा (having given/paid) च (and) वर्धयेत् (one should grow/reinvest). अवशिष्टं (leftover/residual) धनं (wealth/cash) यत् स्यात् (whatever may be) प्रवाहः (flow) सः (that) प्रकीर्तितः (is declared).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 12 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v12">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">अनुसन्धानविस्तारौ पूँजीव्ययौ विचिन्तयेत्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">भाटकं ऋणवद् ग्राह्यं लाभानां शुद्धये बुधैः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> For the purification of true earnings, the wise must recategorize research and development as capital expenses and operating lease commitments must be treated as debt.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">अनुसन्धान-विस्तारौ (research and expansion/development) पूँजी-व्ययौ (as capital expenses) विचिन्तयेत् (one should consider). भाटकं (rent/operating lease) ऋण-वत् (like debt) ग्राह्यं (should be grasped) लाभानां (of earnings) शुद्धये (for the purification) बुधैः (by the wise).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 13 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v13">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">कराणां द्विविधं मानं सीमान्तं च प्रभावकम्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">सीमान्तं शाश्वतं ज्ञेयं मूल्याङ्कनविशारदैः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> There is a twofold measure of corporate taxes: the effective rate and the marginal rate. Valuation experts must know to use the marginal tax rate for forecasting long-term, perpetual cash flows.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">कराणां (of taxes) द्वि-विधं (twofold) मानं (measure) सीमान्तं (marginal) च (and) प्रभावकम् (effective). सीमान्तं (the marginal rate) शाश्वतं (as perpetual/long-term) ज्ञेयं (is to be known) मूल्याङ्कन-विशारदैः (by the experts in valuation).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 14 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v14">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">पूँजीव्ययात् त्यजेद् ह्रासं कार्यशीलधनं तथा।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">एतयोः सङ्गमं प्राहुः पुनर्निधानमुत्तमम्॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The combination of net capital expenditures and changes in non-cash working capital is declared as the true reinvestment need of the firm.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">पूँजी-व्ययात् (from capital expenditures) त्यजेत् (one should subtract) ह्रासं (depreciation) कार्यशील-धनं (working capital) तथा (and also). एतयोः (of these two) सङ्गमं (the union/combination) प्राहुः (they call) पुनर्निधानम् (reinvestment) उत्तमम् (ultimate/true).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 15 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v15">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">स्वपूँजीधनसंस्रावः सम्भाव्यलाभांश उच्यते।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">वितरन्ति न सर्वस्वं सञ्चयार्थं हि वाणिजाः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) is defined as the potential dividend. Businesses rarely distribute everything they can afford, often retaining excess cash.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">स्वपूँजी-धन-संस्रावः (the free cash flow to equity) सम्भाव्य-लाभांशः (potential dividend) उच्यते (is called). वितरन्ति (they distribute) न (not) सर्वस्वं (everything) सञ्चयार्थं (for the sake of accumulation/retention) हि (indeed) वाणिजाः (merchants/businesses).</div>
</details>

## षष्ठाध्यायः - धनप्रवाह-प्रक्षेपणम् (Chapter 6: Forecasting Cash Flows)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 16 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v16">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">अतीतो न प्रमाणं स्याद् भवितव्यस्य निर्णये।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">पुनर्निधानमानेन लाभेनापि च वर्धते॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> Historical growth is not a reliable measure of future growth. Sustainable expected growth is driven by the firm's reinvestment rate and its return on invested capital.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">अतीत: (the past) न (not) प्रमाणं (proof/measure) स्यात् (should be) भवितव्यस्य (of the future) निर्णये (in the determination). पुनर्निधान-मानेन (by the measure of reinvestment) लाभेन (by the return/profit) अपि च (and also) वर्धते (it grows).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 17 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v17">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">यावत्कालमतिशयो लाभानां विद्यते भुवि।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">तावदेव हि विज्ञेयस्तीव्रवृद्धेस्तु कालकः॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> The high-growth period lasts only as long as the firm can sustain returns greater than the cost of capital before competition erodes them.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text">यावत्-कालम् (for as long a time) अतिशयः (excess) लाभानां (of returns) विद्यते (exists) भुवि (in the world/market). तावत् एव (only for that long) हि (indeed) विज्ञेयः (is to be known) तीव्र-वृद्धेः (of high growth) तु (indeed) कालकः (the period).</div>
</details>

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः 18 (Anuṣṭubh)</p>

## एकोनविंशोऽध्यायः - सङ्कट-मूल्य-विचारः (Chapter 19: The Cost of Distress)

  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">सर्वदा चलिता संस्था इति भ्रान्तिं परित्यजेत्।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">सङ्कटे सति नाशः स्यान्मूल्ये तच्च विचिन्तयेत्॥</span>
</div>
<div class="prose-text"><strong>Translation:</strong> Abandon the illusion of infinite life; explicitly consider the risk of ruin in distress.</div>

<details>
<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>

</details>

<summary>पदार्थः (Word-for-word)</summary>
