<details>
<summary>ग्राह्य-त्याज्य-विवेकः / Keep and avoid</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **hash nodes and keys onto a ring**: key belongs to the next node  
२. **many virtual nodes per box**: smooth small or mixed fleets  
३. **only nearby ranges move**: remainder maps reshuffle almost all  
४. **place successors as replicas**: document repair separately  
५. **hash a stable business key**: cookie stickiness breaks on peer change  
६. **split celebrity keys**: hashing does not cool a hot key  
७. **share algorithm vnode count and view**: or clients route differently  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- naive remainder on changing N  
- different ring views in one fleet  
- expect hashing to fix hot keys
