### What this post is about
**Durable workflows** persist long-running business process state so execution resumes correctly after crashes deploys and waits. They host the saga pattern. They are not a substitute for a two-line request.

### Topics
1. Memory-only long jobs die  
2. Event history as truth  
3. Idempotent activities  
4. Durable timers and signals  
5. Replay-safe versioning  
6. Saga compensation hosted  
7. Step-level visibility  
8. Worker fleets and lag scale  
9. Not for simple request-response  
10. Saga versus engine versus leader  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close  

English minimizable. Pure padya.

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **persist event history**: rebuild state  
२. **idempotent activities and heartbeats**: at-least-once workers  
३. **durable timers and signals**: no thread-sleep waits  
४. **version open histories safely**: replay compatible  
५. **map compensations to activities**: saga-aware  
६. **show waiting step and errors**: support visibility  
७. **scale workers on lag**: bulkhead task queues  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- sleep weeks in a request thread for business waits  
- change workflow code breaking replay of open runs  
- workflow engine for every two-line update
