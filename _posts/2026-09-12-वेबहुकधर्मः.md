**जालाह्वानम्** is a signed outbound call to a peer you do not own. The post title keeps the series name.

3. Bound retries
4. Time out the peer
5. Method of a call
6. One fruit once
7. Rotate the secret
8. Park poison
9. Outbox is birth

11. Five limbs
12. Close

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **verify the mark before the body**: unsigned is a weapon  
२. **bound retries with backoff**: endless retry is a door flood  
३. **time out the peer**: a stuck client holds a worker  
४. **one fruit once**: idempotency on delivery  
५. **rotate the secret**: a dead secret is a stolen door  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- accept unsigned bodies  
- retry forever without a dead letter  
- store the signing secret in the payload
