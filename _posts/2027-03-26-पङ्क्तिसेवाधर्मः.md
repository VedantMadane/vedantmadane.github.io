### What this post is about
Line-service dharma is honest queue work. Hold measured workers on the line, watch line fruit on a beat, stop pace when the line floods and free workers only after empty proof.

### Topics
1. Why serve the line
2. Keep workers measured
3. Method: hold measure watch
4. Watch line fruit
5. Stop pace when line is full
6. Write the line bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Free workers only when empty is proven
9. Not endless-worker pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
पङ्क्तिसेवाधर्मः = dharma of line service (queue workers).
