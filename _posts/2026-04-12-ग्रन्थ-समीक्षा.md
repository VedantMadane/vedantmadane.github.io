<p class="prose-text">This edition sets five verses that distil Elad Gil’s <em>High Growth Handbook</em>: CEO time management, board curation, executive hiring and organizational restructuring. Each śloka advances through a progressively complex meter, from अनुष्टुभ् to शार्दूलविक्रीडितम्.</p>

<!-- Audio sync note: each verse block carries a stable data-verse-id for future timed highlighting. -->

<details>
<summary>विषय-विभागः (Scope)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">
<p><strong>सार-संग्रहः (Keep concepts)</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>नायक-कर्तव्यम् (Role of the CEO) स्वकाल-रक्षा (Time management), भाग-दानम् (Delegation).</li>
  <li>सभा-पतित्वम् (Managing the Board) मन्त्रि-सभायाः योग्य-चयनम् (Curating independent members).</li>
  <li>नियुक्ति-क्रमः (Recruiting &amp; Execs) आगामि-मास-दशकाय योग्य-नेत्रीन् आनीय (Hiring for the next 12-18 months).</li>
  <li>संघटना-नीतिः (Organizational Structure) षण्मासेषु मुहुर्भवन्ति विविधाः संस्था-स्वरूपाः (6-12 month restructure cycles).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>त्याज्य-विषयाः (Skip concepts)</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>कथाः (Lengthy interviews).</li>
  <li>वित्त-तन्त्राणि (US-centric corporate details like 409A).</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details>

## १. उपोद्घातः (Introduction)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः १ (अनुष्टुभ्)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v1">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">एलाद-गिल-विप्रेण रचितं वृद्धि-पुस्तकम् ।</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">त्यक्त्वा कथाः सारमात्रं गृह्यते छन्दसां क्रमैः ॥ १॥</span>
</div>

<details>
<summary>शब्दार्थाः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">
<p><strong>एलाद-गिल-विप्रेण</strong> – By the wise Elad Gil (Instrumental)</p>
<p><strong>रचितं</strong> – Composed</p>
<p><strong>वृद्धि-पुस्तकम्</strong> – The book of high growth</p>
<p><strong>त्यक्त्वा</strong> – Having abandonedskipped (Root 'tyaj' + 'ktvā')</p>
<p><strong>कथाः</strong> – The storiesinterviews</p>
<p><strong>सारमात्रं</strong> – Only the essence</p>
<p><strong>गृह्यते</strong> – Is grasped (Passive)</p>
<p><strong>छन्दसां</strong> – Of the meters</p>
<p><strong>क्रमैः</strong> – Through the sequences</p>
</div>
</details>

## २. नायक-कर्तव्यम् (The Role of the CEO)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः २ (उपजाति)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v2">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">स्वकाल-रक्षा खलु मुख्य-नीतिः</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">कार्येषु चान्येषु च भाग-दानम् ।</span><br />
  <span data-line="3" data-start="" data-end="">स्वयञ्च संस्था-पति-कर्म कुर्यात्</span><br />
  <span data-line="4" data-start="" data-end="">नाना-जनानां खलु मार्ग-दर्शी ॥ २॥</span>
</div>

<details>
<summary>शब्दार्थाः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">
<p><strong>स्वकाल-रक्षा</strong> – Protection of one's own time</p>
<p><strong>खलु</strong> – Indeed</p>
<p><strong>मुख्य-नीतिः</strong> – Primary strategy</p>
<p><strong>कार्येषु</strong> – In tasks</p>
<p><strong>अन्येषु</strong> – Unto others</p>
<p><strong>भाग-दानम्</strong> – Delegation</p>
<p><strong>संस्था-पति-कर्म</strong> – Duties of the CEO</p>
<p><strong>कुर्यात्</strong> – Should do</p>
<p><strong>नाना-जनानां</strong> – Of various employees</p>
<p><strong>मार्ग-दर्शी</strong> – The guidepath-shower</p>
</div>
</details>

## ३. सभा-पतित्वम् (Managing the Board)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः ३ (इन्द्रवज्रा)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v3">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">मन्त्रि-सभायाः खलु योग्य-चयनम्</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">ज्ञानेन युक्ताः न तु वित्त-मात्रात् ।</span><br />
  <span data-line="3" data-start="" data-end="">संस्था-विकासाय च मन्त्रणा सा</span><br />
  <span data-line="4" data-start="" data-end="">कार्या सदा वृद्धि-पथं गताय ॥ ३॥</span>
</div>

<details>
<summary>शब्दार्थाः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">
<p><strong>मन्त्रि-सभायाः</strong> – Of the Board of Directors</p>
<p><strong>योग्य-चयनम्</strong> – Proper selection</p>
<p><strong>ज्ञानेन युक्ताः</strong> – Endowed with knowledgeexperience</p>
<p><strong>न तु वित्त-मात्रात्</strong> – Not merely from wealth (VCs)</p>
<p><strong>संस्था-विकासाय</strong> – For company development</p>
<p><strong>मन्त्रणा</strong> – Advicedeliberation</p>
<p><strong>कार्या</strong> – Should be done</p>
<p><strong>सदा</strong> – Always</p>
<p><strong>वृद्धि-पथं गताय</strong> – For the one on the path of growth</p>
</div>
</details>

## ४. नियुक्ति-क्रमः (Recruiting and Executives)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः ४ (वसन्ततिलका)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v4">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">आगामि-मास-दशकाय हि योग्य-नेत्रीन्</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">आनीय कार्य-निपुणान् खलु नूतनांश्च ।</span><br />
  <span data-line="3" data-start="" data-end="">प्राचीन-मित्र-निवहं यदि काल-हीनम्</span><br />
  <span data-line="4" data-start="" data-end="">त्यक्त्वा नवैः सह युजेत् खलु वृद्धि-काले ॥ ४॥</span>
</div>

<details>
<summary>शब्दार्थाः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">
<p><strong>आगामि-मास-दशकाय</strong> – For the coming 10+ months (12-18 months horizon)</p>
<p><strong>योग्य-नेत्रीन्</strong> – Capable executives</p>
<p><strong>आनीय</strong> – Having hired</p>
<p><strong>कार्य-निपुणान्</strong> – Function experts</p>
<p><strong>नूतनान्</strong> – New ones</p>
<p><strong>प्राचीन-मित्र-निवहं</strong> – The group of old friends (Old-timers)</p>
<p><strong>काल-हीनम्</strong> – Lacking the times (Unscalable)</p>
<p><strong>त्यक्त्वा</strong> – Having let go</p>
<p><strong>नवैः सह</strong> – With the new ones</p>
<p><strong>युजेत्</strong> – Should buildjoin</p>
<p><strong>वृद्धि-काले</strong> – In hyper-growth</p>
</div>
</details>

## ५. संघटना-नीतिः (Organizational Structure)

<p class="verse-topic">श्लोकः ५ (शार्दूलविक्रीडितम्)</p>
<div class="sanskrit-text sanskrit-verse-lines" data-verse-id="v5">
  <span data-line="1" data-start="" data-end="">षण्मासेषु मुहुर्भवन्ति विविधाः संस्था-स्वरूपा नवाः</span><br />
  <span data-line="2" data-start="" data-end="">नास्त्येकं खलु पूर्ण-रूपमथवा निर्दोष-यन्त्रं भुवि ।</span><br />
  <span data-line="3" data-start="" data-end="">ये कुर्युः खलु निर्णयं जव-युतं तेष्वेव कार्यं न्यसेत्</span><br />
  <span data-line="4" data-start="" data-end="">सिकतोपत्यक-देश-तन्त्र-विधिना संस्था प्रकर्षं गता ॥ ५॥</span>
</div>

<details>
<summary>शब्दार्थाः (Word-for-word)</summary>
<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">
<p><strong>षण्मासेषु</strong> – In six months</p>
<p><strong>मुहुर्भवन्ति</strong> – Repeatedly become</p>
<p><strong>संस्था-स्वरूपाः</strong> – Org structures</p>
<p><strong>नास्त्येकं पूर्ण-रूपम्</strong> – There is no single perfect form</p>
<p><strong>निर्दोष-यन्त्रं</strong> – Flawless machine</p>
<p><strong>ये कुर्युः निर्णयं</strong> – Those who would make the decision (Tie-breakers)</p>
<p><strong>जव-युतं</strong> – With speed</p>
<p><strong>तेष्वेव कार्यं न्यसेत्</strong> – Task should be entrusted to them</p>
<p><strong>सिकतोपत्यक-देश-तन्त्र-विधिना</strong> – By the rules of the Silicon Valley ('Sikatā' = SiliconSand, 'Upatyakā' = Valley)</p>
<p><strong>संस्था प्रकर्षं गता</strong> – The organization reaches excellence</p>
</div>
</details>
