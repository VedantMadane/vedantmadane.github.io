### What this post is about
Queue-guard dharma is honest depth custody on the line. Guard queue depth, watch queue fruit on a beat, stop write when the queue is full and never treat endless queue as safe.

### Topics
1. Why guard the queue
2. Guard queue do not forget
3. Method: hold guard measure watch
4. Watch queue fruit
5. Stop write when full
6. Write the queue bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Release queue when empty is proven
9. Not endless-queue pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
पङ्क्तिपालधर्मः = dharma of queue-guard (queue depth custody).
