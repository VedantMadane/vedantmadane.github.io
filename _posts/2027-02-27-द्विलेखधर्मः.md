### What this post is about
Dual write is the craft of keeping two stores honest under one business fact. The path fails when one side succeeds and the other is forgotten, or when clients see a half-committed world.

### Topics
1. Why dual write needs dharma
2. Order before the pen
3. Method: both or neither
4. Idempotent retry
5. Never half-publish
6. Compare on a clock
7. Outbox as helper limb
8. Failure modes named
9. Read-your-writes care
10. Five limbs of dual write
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
द्विलेखधर्मः = dharma of writing to two stores as one truth.

### English
Minimizable grammar maps; Sanskrit-first.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **fix write order before runtime**: order  
२. **commit both or publish neither**: atom  
३. **retry with the same key**: idem  
४. **compare stores on a clock**: diff  
५. **name half-write as failure**: signal  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Publish after first store only  
- Different order per code path  
- Silent skip of the second write  
- No periodic parity check  
- Treat dual write as free consistency
