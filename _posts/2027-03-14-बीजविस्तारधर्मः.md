### What this post is about
Seed fan-out dharma is honest parallel expand. Hold many seeds, grow them in measure, compare fruit on a beat and never let one bad seed fan the whole field.

### Topics
1. Why fan many seeds
2. Expand in measure not rush
3. Method: measure many compare
4. Compare fruit on a beat
5. Kill bad seed path on fault
6. Write the expand bound first
7. Name the failure
8. Expand only when proven
9. Not endless fan pride
10. Five limbs
11. Close
12. Seal

### Sanskrit title
बीजविस्तारधर्मः = dharma of measured seed fan-out.

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **hold many seeds under one plan**: many  
२. **expand each seed in measure**: measure  
३. **compare fruit on a schedule**: compare  
४. **kill a bad seed path alone**: kill  
५. **write the expand bound first**: bound  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- Fan all seeds at once without bound  
- No fruit compare across seeds  
- Let one bad seed fan the full field  
- Hide seed faults from operators  
- No written expand limit
