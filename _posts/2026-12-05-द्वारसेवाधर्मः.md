### What this post is about
**API gateways** present a controlled external front door for routing identity coarse limits and edge policy without becoming a business-logic monolith.

### Topics covered
1. Centralize edge policy  
2. North-south not east-west  
3. Authn edge authz service  
4. Routing versioning canary  
5. Rate limit and WAF  
6. Careful gateway retries  
7. Edge vs upstream latency  
8. Keep the gateway thin  
9. HA and config as code  
10. Mesh vs gateway vs token bucket  

### Sanskrit title
**द्वारसेवाधर्मः** = *dvāra-sevā-dharmaḥ* "dharma of the door-service".

<div class="prose-text" markdown="1">

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **use gateway for N-S edge**: mesh for E-W  
२. **authenticate at door**: authorize in service too  
३. **route/canary at edge**: progressive delivery  
४. **coarse rate limit+WAF**: public mouth  
५. **avoid amplifying retries**: idempotent only  
६. **split edge vs upstream metrics**: clear blame  
७. **keep business logic out**: thin policy box  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- put core business workflows only in the gateway  
- gateway retries all POSTs without keys  
- one blended latency SLO for edge+app
