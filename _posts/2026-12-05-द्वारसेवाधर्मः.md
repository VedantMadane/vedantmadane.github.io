### What this post is about
**द्वारसेवा** is a thin edge that proves identity, bounds rate and routes coarsely. It is not a second product monolith and not a per-client face.

1. Door not womb  
2. Auth rate route  
3. Keep it thin  
4. Stable outer contract  
5. Method of the edge  
6. Deadlines  
7. No secret dump  
8. Not a BFF  
9. Trace every ask  
10. Gateway versus face versus strangler  
11. Five limbs  
12. Close

### ग्राह्यम् / Keep
१. **identity rate and coarse route at the door**: money rules in core  
२. **keep the gateway thin**: no product assembly  
३. **version the outer path**: do not break old clients  
४. **parent deadline at the edge**: smaller budget below  
५. **do not log inner secrets at the door**: path not heart  
६. **leave client shape to a face-service**: policy stays here  
७. **trace every ask**: no blind drop  

### त्याज्यम् / Avoid
- one fat gateway that builds every mobile payload  
- money invariants only in gateway plugins  
- silent field rename on the public path
